## Answer

 ## **Gastroenteritis**

**Gastroenteritis** is inflammation or infection of the stomach and intestines. It may be caused by viruses or bacteria; norovirus is a common cause. Acute gastroenteritis is often defined as **at least three loose or watery stools in 24 hours**, with or without vomiting, fever, or abdominal pain, and usually lasts less than one week. (1) (2)

### Symptoms
- Sudden-onset **diarrhea, vomiting, or both**
- Fever
- Headache or muscle aches
- Abdominal pain or cramping
- Loss of appetite (3)

Viral gastroenteritis may also cause mild, diffuse abdominal tenderness. (4)

### Spread
Infections can spread through contact with an infected person or contaminated surfaces, or through contaminated food and liquids. (1)

### Dehydration
Severe diarrhea and vomiting can cause **dehydration**, which may lead to thirst, tiredness, dizziness, confusion, and dark-yellow urine. Severe dehydration can be life-threatening, particularly in babies and young children. (3)

### Treatment
Most children and babies improve without specific treatment. Severe dehydration may require hospital treatment with fluids given through an intravenous line. Antibiotics do not help viral gastroenteritis; they are sometimes used for bacterial gastroenteritis depending on the infection and its severity. (5)
