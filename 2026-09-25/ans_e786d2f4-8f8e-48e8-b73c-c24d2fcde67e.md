## Answer

 **Severe TTN requiring invasive mechanical ventilation is extremely rare.** Infants with TTN usually require no more than supplemental oxygen or noninvasive support; mechanical ventilation should prompt reassessment for alternative diagnoses such as **sepsis, pneumonia, respiratory distress syndrome, congenital heart disease, or air leak**. (1) (2) (3) (4)

Symptoms can persist for up to **72 hours in severe TTN**, but persistent or escalating respiratory failure is atypical. (5)
