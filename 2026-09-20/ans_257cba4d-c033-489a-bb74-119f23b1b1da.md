## Answer

 **Aspirin dose depends on the indication:**

- **Primary prevention:** 75–100 mg once daily. (1)  
- **Long-term secondary prevention of cardiovascular disease:** commonly 75–100 mg once daily; 325 mg may be used in selected patients. (2)  
- **Acute occlusive cardiovascular event:** loading dose at least 162 mg, preferably 325 mg, for rapid antithrombotic effect. (3)  
- **Stable ischemic heart disease:** 75–100 mg once daily. (4)  

Higher doses may increase bleeding risk, particularly above 325 mg/day. (2)
