## Answer

 **Aspirin dosing:**

- **Maintenance:** 75–100 mg once daily for most indications; **81 mg daily** is commonly used in the United States. (1) (2)
- **Loading dose:** At least **160 mg** when rapid platelet inhibition is needed; a typical loading dose is **325 mg**. (1) (2)
- **Occasionally:** 325 mg daily may be used for maintenance in some patients after stroke. (2)

Higher doses generally do not improve efficacy and increase dose-related gastrointestinal and bleeding risks. (1)
