## Answer

 The provided references support the following **newborn/term-infant** ranges:

| Age/status | Normal respiratory rate | Normal heart rate |
|---|---:|---:|
| **Healthy term infant** | Approximately **40–60 breaths/min** (1) | **120–160 beats/min** (2) |
| **First hour after birth** | May be higher; approximately **50% of healthy newborns have rates >60 breaths/min** (1) | Not specified in the provided references |
| **During sleep** | Not specified | May decrease to **about 85 beats/min** in term infants (2) |

A respiratory rate **>60 breaths/min**, especially with retractions, nasal flaring, or grunting, is considered a sign of respiratory distress (3).

Normal rates for older age groups cannot be found in the provided references.
