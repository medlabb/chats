## Answer

 ## Management of malignant hyperthermia

Treat immediately when there is a rising **ETCO₂ despite increased minute ventilation**, especially with tachycardia, hyperthermia, muscle rigidity, or ECG changes suggestive of hyperkalemia. (1)

1. **Call for help** and bring the MH treatment cart. Notify the surgeon and terminate the operation as soon as possible. (2)

2. **Stop triggering agents**
   - Discontinue all volatile anesthetics and succinylcholine.
   - Increase fresh-gas flow to **≥10 L/min** with **100% oxygen**.
   - Replace the disposable breathing circuit and reservoir bag; insert activated charcoal filters in the inspiratory and expiratory limbs if available. The anesthesia machine itself need not be changed.
   - If surgery must continue, use intravenous non-triggering anesthesia, such as propofol with opioids as needed. (2)

3. **Optimize ventilation**
   - Hyperventilate, increasing the ventilation rate and/or tidal volume—up to approximately three times the normal minute ventilation.
   - Intubate using intravenous anesthetic/sedative-hypnotic agents and **nondepolarizing** neuromuscular blockers if paralysis is required. (2)

4. **Give dantrolene immediately**
   - Initial dose: **2.5 mg/kg IV rapidly**.
   - Repeat **2.5 mg/kg IV every 5 minutes** if necessary, up to a total of **10 mg/kg**, until clinical signs—particularly ETCO₂—improve. (3)
   - Dantrolene reduces abnormal calcium release from skeletal muscle and is the only known specific therapy for MH. (4)

5. **Treat complications and monitor**
   - Assess for and treat **hyperkalemia**.
   - Treat acidosis and other abnormalities as indicated.
   - Monitor arterial/venous blood gases, core temperature, CK, urine output and color, electrolytes, and coagulation parameters. (3)
   - Manage arrhythmias with correction of acidosis and hyperkalemia, together with standard ACLS protocols. (5)

6. **Post-crisis care**
   - Admit to intensive care for supportive management and close monitoring for at least **24 hours**.
   - Continue dantrolene for **24–48 hours**. (5)
