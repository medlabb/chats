## Answer

 **Desmoplastic small round cell tumor (DSRCT)** is a rare soft-tissue sarcoma characterized by the translocation **t(11;22), producing an EWSR1::WT1 fusion**. This specific translocation helps distinguish it from **Ewing sarcoma** and other small round blue cell tumors. (1)

DSRCT may be included in the differential diagnosis of small round blue cell tumors involving bone and soft tissue. Cytogenetic or molecular genetic testing is generally required to establish the diagnosis in such diagnostically challenging cases. (2)
