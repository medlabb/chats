```
When to stop anticoagulant in ulcerative colitis flare?
```

- - -

## Answer

 - **Hospitalized ulcerative colitis/IBD flare without severe bleeding:** anticoagulant VTE prophylaxis is recommended. (1)  
- **Severe gastrointestinal bleeding:** use **mechanical thromboprophylaxis instead of anticoagulant prophylaxis**. (1)  
- If anticoagulation is being used to treat an acute pulmonary embolism and there is significant active bleeding, it may be **deferred until bleeding is controlled**. (2)  
- After a gastrointestinal bleed, anticoagulation is generally restarted once hemostasis is achieved and bleeding risk is acceptable; the optimal timing cited is **about 7–14 days**, depending on thrombotic and rebleeding risks. (3)

The references do not specify an exact stopping rule for every ulcerative-colitis flare or for a particular anticoagulant. Decisions should be individualized with the treating clinician, especially if anticoagulation is for a prior clot, atrial fibrillation, or another high-risk indication.
