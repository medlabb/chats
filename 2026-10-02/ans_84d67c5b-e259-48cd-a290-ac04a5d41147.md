```
Why is the 7th nerve involved in the internal capsule stroke
```

- - -

## Answer

 The **facial nerve itself usually isn’t damaged**. A stroke in the **genu of the internal capsule** can affect the brain’s corticobulbar pathways that control facial muscles, causing facial weakness on the opposite side of the body (1, 2). The **lower face is typically more affected than the forehead**, because the forehead receives input from both motor cortices, while the lower face receives input mainly from the opposite cortex (2).
