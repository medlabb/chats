## Answer

 If you mean **migraine**, options differ for treating an attack versus preventing future attacks.

**Acute treatment options**
- **NSAIDs:** naproxen 220–550 mg by mouth twice daily; ibuprofen 400 mg by mouth every 3–4 hours; diclofenac potassium 50 mg by mouth. (1)
- **Triptans:** sumatriptan 50–100 mg orally, 20 mg intranasally, or 3–6 mg subcutaneously; rizatriptan 5–10 mg orally; naratriptan 2.5 mg orally; zolmitriptan 2.5 mg orally or 5 mg intranasally. Other listed options include almotriptan 12.5 mg, eletriptan 40–80 mg, and frovatriptan 2.5 mg orally. (1)
- **Gepants:** rimegepant 75 mg orally; ubrogepant 50 or 100 mg orally (a second dose may be taken after 2 hours if needed); zavegepant 10 mg intranasally once in 24 hours. (1)
- **Lasmiditan:** 50, 100, or 200 mg orally. (1)
- **Dopamine-receptor antagonists as adjuncts:** metoclopramide 10 mg or prochlorperazine 10 mg orally; injectable options listed include metoclopramide 10 mg IV or prochlorperazine 10 mg IV. (2; 1)
- **Dihydroergotamine:** 1 mg IV, IM, or subcutaneously at onset; the table allows repeat dosing hourly, up to 3 mg/day and 6 mg/week. (1)
- **Neuromodulation:** sTMS—two pulses at onset, followed by two further pulses; nVNS—two 120-second doses; remote electrical neuromodulation—30–45 minutes; supraorbital nerve stimulation—60 minutes; eCOT-NS—30–60 minutes. (3; 1)

**Preventive options**
- **Propranolol** 40–120 mg twice daily; **metoprolol** 25–100 mg twice daily; **amitriptyline** 10–75 mg nightly; **venlafaxine** 75–150 mg/day. (4)
- **Topiramate** 25–200 mg/day; **valproate** 400–600 mg twice daily. (4)
- **CGRP-pathway treatments:** eptinezumab 100 or 300 mg IV every 12 weeks; erenumab 70 or 140 mg subcutaneously monthly; fremanezumab 225 mg monthly or 675 mg every 3 months subcutaneously; galcanezumab 240 mg loading dose, then 120 mg monthly subcutaneously. (4)
- **Other listed preventives:** rimegepant 75 mg every other day; atogepant 10, 30, or 60 mg daily; candesartan 4–24 mg daily; melatonin 3–12 mg nightly. (4)
- For **chronic migraine**, onabotulinum toxin type A is listed at 155 units. (4)
- Preventive neuromodulation options listed include sTMS 4–24 pulses/day, nVNS 120-second treatments 2–3 times daily, remote electrical neuromodulation 45 minutes every other day, and transcutaneous supraorbital stimulation 20 minutes daily. (4)

The references list these as treatment options and typical doses; they do not identify which is appropriate for an individual. They also note that treatment should be individualized and that preventive medicines are generally started low and increased gradually. (4; 5)
