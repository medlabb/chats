## Answer

 Propranolol **reduces heart rate and myocardial contractility** and is listed for symptomatic hypertrophic subaortic stenosis, but the references do **not** establish a role for treating biventricular hypertrophy in a newborn after TTTS (1; 2).

The references also flag **heart failure as a contraindication** in an infant-treatment section and advise caution in compensated heart failure; they note that propranolol’s efficacy in heart failure has not been demonstrated (3; 4). Thus, these references do not support prescribing it for the scenario described. TTTS-specific treatment guidance cannot be found in the selected references.
