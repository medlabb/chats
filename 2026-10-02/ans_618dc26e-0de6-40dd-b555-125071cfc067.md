## Answer

 **Pneumonia** is an infection of the pulmonary parenchyma—the lung tissue. It is commonly classified as community-acquired (CAP), hospital-acquired (HAP), or ventilator-associated (VAP). (1)

Aspiration pneumonia is considered part of the continuum between CAP and HAP. The references estimate it accounts for 5–15% of CAP cases. (1)
