```
1- 7 years old girl presented with breast development and pubic hair, breast tanner 3 and pubic hair tanner 2 , normal FSH, IH , brain mri normal, high estradiol ?
A. Central precocious puberty
B. Peripheral precocious puberty

2- A 7-year-old girl presents with secondary sexual characteristics, including breast development (Tanner stage 3) and pubic hair growth (Tanner stage 2). Laboratory workup reveals an elevated estradiol level, but normal FSH and LH levels. A brain MRI is reported as normal. Her bone age advanced to 9 years old
What is the most likely diagnosis?
A. Central precocious puberty (CPP)
B. Peripheral precocious puberty (PPP)
```

- - -

## Answer

 1. **B. Peripheral precocious puberty (PPP)** is the better fit: elevated estradiol with normal gonadotropins is more consistent with sex-steroid production independent of the hypothalamic-pituitary-ovarian axis than with the increased gonadotropin activity characteristic of central precocious puberty (1, 2).

2. **B. Peripheral precocious puberty (PPP)** is the most likely choice for the same reason. Advanced bone age supports true precocious puberty, but does not by itself distinguish central from peripheral causes (1).

The reference describes PPP as having **suppressed gonadotropins** alongside elevated estradiol; “normal” LH/FSH results alone do not fully establish the mechanism. A GnRH/agonist stimulation test is listed among the evaluation tests (2).
