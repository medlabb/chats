## Answer

 **Animal bites** are heavily contaminated with bacteria. Dog-bite wounds may be left open for wound care, but many can be properly cleaned and closed without infection. Bite wounds generally need careful irrigation; wounds with devitalized or contaminated tissue may also need debridement. (1) (2)

For bites to the **hand or upper extremity**, irrigation and debridement are required; dog and cat bites may involve *Pasteurella* bacteria. Elevating an affected extremity can help with healing. (3) (1)

Check **tetanus immunization status** after a penetrating injury. The reference identifies wounds with saliva contamination as tetanus-prone and provides booster and immune-globulin guidance in Table 24-1. (2) (4)
