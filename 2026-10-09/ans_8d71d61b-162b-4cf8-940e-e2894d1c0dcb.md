## Answer

 For **routine dental local anesthesia**, use a **commercially premixed** solution:

- **Lidocaine 2% + epinephrine 1:100,000** is preferred.
- Use **1:50,000** when more pronounced hemostasis is needed. (1)

**Formula for a 1:200,000 concentration**—specified in the reference for **epidural anesthesia**:

> **0.1 mL epinephrine 1:1,000 + 20 mL lidocaine = epinephrine 5 micrograms/mL (1:200,000)** (2)

Lidocaine 2% contains **20 mg/mL**. Do not exceed **7 mg/kg of lidocaine**, up to **500 mg**, and for dental use do not exceed **0.2 mg epinephrine per appointment**. (3)

The references do **not** provide a validated bedside mixing formula for preparing 1:50,000 or 1:100,000 epinephrine with lidocaine 2%; use a licensed premixed product rather than improvising the concentration.
