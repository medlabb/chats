## Answer

 For a **13-day-old neonate with critical valvar aortic stenosis and cardiogenic shock**:

1. **Begin immediate stabilization:** provide respiratory support as needed, establish vascular access and continuous monitoring, correct hypoglycemia, electrolyte/acid–base abnormalities, and support hypotension/poor perfusion with an inotropic agent. (1)  
2. **Start prostaglandin E1 (alprostadil) promptly** to maintain ductal patency. This can allow the right ventricle to support systemic circulation while definitive treatment is arranged. (2)  
3. **Obtain urgent echocardiography** to assess anatomy and cardiac function, but do not delay stabilization for a detailed evaluation in a patient with shock. (3) (4)  
4. **Proceed urgently to catheter-based balloon aortic valvuloplasty**, the first-line definitive intervention for severe/critical valvar aortic stenosis; it can be lifesaving in critical aortic stenosis. (5) (2)
