## Answer

 **E. Wiskott–Aldrich syndrome**

The combination of **eczema, recurrent otitis/infections, and severe thrombocytopenia in a young boy** is characteristic of Wiskott–Aldrich syndrome, an X-linked disorder involving atopic dermatitis, thrombocytopenic purpura, and susceptibility to infection. (1) (2)
