```
Do Gilbert syndrome presents in neonatal period as jaundice
```

- - -

## Answer

 Yes. **Gilbert syndrome usually does not manifest until after the second decade of life**, but **some neonates may develop unconjugated hyperbilirubinemia (jaundice)**, attributed to diminished bilirubin uptake. (1)
