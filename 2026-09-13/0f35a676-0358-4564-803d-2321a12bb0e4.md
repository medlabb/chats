```
A 4-year-old child weighing 18 kg presents with diabetic ketoacidosis. His serum sodium is 138 mEq/L and serum glucose is 600 mg/dL. Using the standard correction of serum sodium for hyperglycemia, what is the corrected serum sodium concentration if sodium increases by 1.6 mEq/L for every 100 mg/dL increase in glucose above 100 mg/dL?
```

- - -

## Answer

 **Corrected serum sodium** = 138 + 1.6 × [(600 − 100) / 100]  
= 138 + 8  
= **146 mEq/L** (1)
