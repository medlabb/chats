## Answer

 **Aspirin dose:**

- **Maintenance:** 75–325 mg once daily; **75–100 mg daily** is recommended for most indications, commonly **81 mg daily** in the U.S. (1; 2)
- **Loading dose when rapid platelet inhibition is needed:** at least **160 mg**; commonly **325 mg**. (1; 2)
- **Acute coronary syndrome:** **150–325 mg orally** using a non-enteric-coated preparation, followed by **75–100 mg daily**. (3; 4)
