## Answer

 **Aspirin dose:**

- **Maintenance:** typically **75–100 mg once daily**; **81 mg daily** is common in the United States. (1)  
- **Usual range:** **75–325 mg once daily**. (2)  
- **Loading dose when rapid platelet inhibition is needed:** **150–325 mg orally**; a dose of at least **160 mg** is recommended. (3)  
- **Intravenous loading dose:** **75–250 mg**. (3)  

For most indications, **75–100 mg daily** is preferred because higher doses do not improve efficacy and increase dose-related adverse effects. (2)
