```
Which of the following is the most common cause of apnea in adults?
​A: Central nervous system depression
​B: Obstructive sleep apnea
​C: Neuromuscular disease
​D: Chest wall deformity
```

- - -

## Answer

 **B: Obstructive sleep apnea** — it is the most common type of sleep apnea in adults and is more common than central sleep apnea. (1) (2)
