## Answer

 **Aspirin dosing:**

- **Usual maintenance:** 75–325 mg once daily; **75–100 mg daily** is recommended for most indications. (1)
- **Loading dose:** 150–325 mg orally, preferably non-enteric-coated, when rapid platelet inhibition is needed. (2)  
- In the United States, **81 mg daily** is the most common maintenance dose. (3)
- **Contraindications noted:** severe active bleeding or aspirin allergy. (2)
