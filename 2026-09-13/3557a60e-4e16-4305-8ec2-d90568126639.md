## Answer

 **Aspirin dose depends on the indication:**

- **Primary cardiovascular prevention:** 75–100 mg once daily (often 81 mg). (1) (2)
- **Secondary cardiovascular prevention:** commonly 75–100 mg once daily; 325 mg may be used in selected patients. (3)
- **Acute coronary syndrome or acute occlusive cardiovascular event:** an initial loading dose of 162–325 mg, preferably regular non-enteric-coated aspirin; maintenance is generally 75–100 mg once daily. (4) (5)
- **Pain or fever:** 325 mg–1 g every 4–6 hours as needed; usual maximum 4 g/day. (6)

Higher doses may increase bleeding risk, particularly above 325 mg/day. (3)
