```
A male infant is born at 28 weeks' gestation with a birth weight of 1,050 g. At 12 hours of life, he develops respiratory distress and requires mechanical ventilation. His serum sodium is 151 mEq/L, urine output during the previous 6 hours is 0.8 mL/kg/h, and his daily fluid requirement is estimated at 120 mL/kg/day. If the infant receives 70% of his fluid requirement enterally and the remainder intravenously, how many milliliters of IV fluid should he receive during the first 24 hours?
```

- - -

## Answer

 **IV fluid volume:**  
\(120 \text{ mL/kg/day} \times 1.05 \text{ kg} = 126 \text{ mL/day}\)

Enteral intake provides 70%, so IV fluid provides 30%:

\(126 \times 0.30 = \boxed{37.8\text{ mL}}\)

The infant should receive **37.8 mL of IV fluid during the first 24 hours** (approximately **38 mL**). Fluid from enteral intake must be accounted for when determining the IV volume. (1)
