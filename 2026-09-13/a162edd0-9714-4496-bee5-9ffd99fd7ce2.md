## Answer

 **Aspirin dosing:**

- **Usual maintenance dose:** **75–100 mg orally once daily**; 81 mg daily is commonly used in the United States. (1)
- **Acceptable general range:** **75–325 mg once daily**. Higher doses are not more effective and may cause more dose-related adverse effects. (2)
- **When rapid platelet inhibition is needed:** give an initial **160 mg or more**; for acute coronary syndrome, an initial **150–325 mg orally** (non-enteric-coated) may be used. (2) (3)
- **Intravenous initial dose in acute coronary syndrome:** **75–250 mg IV**. (3)

The appropriate dose depends on the indication and bleeding risk.
