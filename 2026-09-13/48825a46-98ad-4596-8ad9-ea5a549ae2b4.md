## Answer

 **Aspirin dosing depends on the indication:**

- **Usual antiplatelet maintenance:** **75–100 mg orally once daily**; 81 mg daily is commonly used. (1)
- **General dosing range:** **75–325 mg once daily**; higher doses are not more effective and cause more dose-related adverse effects. (2)
- **When rapid platelet inhibition is needed:** an initial dose of at least **160 mg**; for acute coronary syndrome, **150–325 mg orally** of a non-enteric-coated preparation is recommended, followed by **75–100 mg daily**. (2) (3)
- **Intravenous acute coronary syndrome dose:** **75–250 mg IV**. (3)

The appropriate dose depends on the clinical indication and bleeding risk.
