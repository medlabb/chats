## Answer

 **Aspirin dose depends on the indication:**

- **Noncardioembolic TIA or acute ischemic stroke:** 325 mg orally once, followed by **81–325 mg daily**, unless thrombolysis is planned. (1)
- **Secondary prevention after noncardioembolic TIA or ischemic stroke:** **81–325 mg daily**. (2)
- **Primary prevention:** low-dose aspirin **81–100 mg daily** in selected patients, balanced against bleeding risk. (3)
- **Acute migraine:** **650 mg every 4 hours**, limited to 14 days per month; may cause gastrointestinal bleeding or tinnitus. (4)
- **Migraine prophylaxis:** **325 mg daily**. (5)

If aspirin is being considered after alteplase, antiplatelet agents should not be given during the first 24 hours. (1)
