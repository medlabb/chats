## Answer

 **Ampicillin-sulbactam IV may be considered only in selected complicated cases requiring systemic/parenteral antibiotics**, such as acute tympanostomy-tube otorrhea with severe symptoms, systemic infection, or immunocompromise. However, the selected reference specifically suggests high-dose **amoxicillin or amoxicillin-clavulanate** and does not establish IV ampicillin-sulbactam as a recommended treatment for routine otitis media. (1)

An oral prodrug, **sultamicillin**, has been used for ear infections, but this does not directly establish the use or dosing of IV ampicillin-sulbactam for otitis media. (2)
