## Answer

 For a **small second-degree burn**:

- Cool it promptly with cool—not icy—water or a cool compress. Don’t put ice directly on the skin or soak the burn for more than 15 minutes. (1, 2)
- Gently wash it with soap and lukewarm water. Apply a thin layer of antibiotic ointment and cover it with a **non-stick bandage**; change the dressing once or twice daily as needed. (rf_86, 2)
- Don’t pop blisters. For pain, you can elevate the burned area and consider over-the-counter acetaminophen or ibuprofen. (rf_86, 2)

Larger, deeper, or burns involving critical body areas should be assessed by a health care provider experienced in burn care. (1)
