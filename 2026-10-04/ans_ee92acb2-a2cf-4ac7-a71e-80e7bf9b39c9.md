## Answer

 For **type 2 diabetes**, medication choice depends on blood-sugar goals, other health conditions, preferences, and cost. Metformin is suggested as initial treatment for many newly diagnosed patients, alongside lifestyle changes, unless it is unsuitable or another medication is specifically indicated (1). It should not be used when eGFR is below 30 mL/min/1.73 m² (1).

- For people with cardiovascular or kidney disease, a GLP-1 receptor agonist or SGLT2 inhibitor with demonstrated benefit may be considered (1).
- For severe or symptomatic hyperglycemia, insulin may be indicated; a GLP-1-based therapy is another option in some cases (2).
- If blood sugar remains above the individualized goal, another medication may be added; the choice depends on factors such as health conditions, weight, low-blood-sugar risk, preferences, and cost (2; 3).
