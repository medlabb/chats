Small for gestational age?

## Answer

 **Small for gestational age (SGA)** means a newborn’s birth weight is below the **10th percentile** for gestational age. (1)

SGA is not the same as fetal growth restriction (FGR): many SGA newborns are constitutionally small, while FGR refers to restricted growth in utero. (1, rf_f6)
