## Answer

 Malaria treatment depends on the **species, illness severity, local drug resistance, and available medicines**. Uncomplicated *P. falciparum* malaria is generally treated with an artemisinin-based combination therapy (ACT); options include artemether-lumefantrine and artesunate-amodiaquine. (1)

**Severe malaria is a medical emergency:** treatment should begin promptly in hospital, usually with intravenous artesunate, with close monitoring. Parenteral treatment is continued for at least 24 hours and until the patient can take oral medicine; an oral regimen is then used to complete treatment. (2) (3)
