## Answer

 **Ketonuria 2+** can occur with:

- **Diabetic ketoacidosis (DKA)** or significant insulin deficiency—especially with diabetes, high glucose, or missed/insufficient insulin. A urine ketone result of **≥2+** is one criterion for DKA, but diagnosis also requires hyperglycemia or a history of diabetes and metabolic acidosis. (1)
- **Fasting or reduced food intake**
- **After exercise**
- **Pregnancy**
- **Dehydration**, which can also cause a false-positive result
- **Alcohol use** or toxic drugs
- **Euglycemic ketoacidosis**, including with **SGLT-2 inhibitor use**, decreased food intake, pregnancy, alcohol use, or liver failure. (2) (3) (4)

If you have **diabetes**, or symptoms such as nausea, vomiting, abdominal pain, marked fatigue, excessive thirst/urination, rapid deep breathing, drowsiness, or fruity-smelling breath, urgent medical assessment is needed because DKA is life-threatening. (1) (5)
