```
What is role of expiratory CT chest in pulmonology
```

## Answer

 **Expiratory CT of the chest** complements inspiratory HRCT by revealing abnormalities that become more apparent during expiration:

- **Air trapping and small-airway disease:** Expiratory images can demonstrate air trapping and heterogeneous lung inflation, including in early disease. This is particularly useful in cystic fibrosis and bronchiolar disorders. (1)
- **Differentiating asthma from bronchiolitis:** A **mosaic attenuation pattern that becomes more prominent on expiration** favors bronchiolitis over severe asthma. Expiratory images should be included when evaluating suspected bronchiolitis. (2)
- **Interstitial lung disease evaluation:** Standard HRCT protocols for ILD typically include sustained end-inspiratory and end-expiratory images, helping characterize the disease pattern. (3)
- **Dynamic central-airway collapse:** Dynamic expiratory CT can demonstrate expiratory collapse in suspected **tracheobronchomalacia**. (4)
- **Emphysema and bronchoscopic lung-volume-reduction planning:** Inspiratory and expiratory HRCT images are used to assess emphysema severity and distribution and to help evaluate fissure integrity and treatment targets. (5)

Thus, expiratory CT is especially valuable for detecting **air trapping, mosaic attenuation, small-airway disease, and dynamic airway collapse**, as well as for selected emphysema-treatment assessments.
