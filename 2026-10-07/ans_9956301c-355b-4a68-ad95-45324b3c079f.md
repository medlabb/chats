```
Clinical MCQ of the Day

A 14-year-old boy presents with poor growth, intermittent abdominal bloating and iron deficiency anemia that has not improved despite adequate oral iron and confirmed adherence. He continues to eat wheat daily.

Testing for suspected celiac disease shows:

* Tissue transglutaminase IgA (tTG-IgA): negative

* Total serum IgA: <5 mg/dL

* Serum IgG and IgM: normal

*Which is the most appropriate next serological test?*

A. Repeat tTG-IgA using the same assay

B. IgA anti-endomysial antibodies

C. IgG antibodies against tissue transglutaminase

D. IgE antibodies against wheat
```

- - -

## Answer

 **C. IgG antibodies against tissue transglutaminase (tTG-IgG).**

Severe IgA deficiency makes the negative tTG-IgA result unreliable. In patients with very low IgA, **IgG-based testing should be performed**, including tTG-IgG; concurrent DGP-IgG may also be considered. (1) (2)
