```
What is role of expiratory CT chest in pulmonology
```

- - -

## Answer

 **Expiratory CT of the chest** is used to reveal abnormalities that may be occult on inspiratory imaging, particularly:

- **Air trapping and heterogeneous ventilation:** Expiratory HRCT can demonstrate unsuspected air trapping and inhomogeneous lung inflation, including in early cystic fibrosis disease. In established CF, it may also show mucus plugging, centrilobular nodules, peribronchial thickening, and bronchiectasis. (1)
- **Small-airway disease and bronchiolitis:** In patients with suspected severe or atypical asthma, expiratory images are included when assessing for bronchiolitis. A **mosaic attenuation pattern that becomes more prominent during expiration** favors bronchiolitis over severe asthma. (2)
- **Tracheobronchomalacia or central-airway collapse:** Dynamic expiratory CT can demonstrate expiratory collapse of the central airways. (3)
- **Interstitial lung disease assessment:** HRCT protocols for ILD commonly acquire images during sustained inspiration and expiration, helping identify associated expiratory abnormalities such as air trapping. (4)
- **Planning bronchoscopic lung-volume-reduction therapy:** Inspiratory and expiratory HRCT is typically required to assess emphysema distribution and severity, fissure integrity, and potential collateral ventilation when selecting candidates and target lobes. (5)

Thus, expiratory CT is particularly valuable for evaluating **air trapping, small-airway disease, bronchiolitis, dynamic airway collapse, and procedural planning in severe emphysema**.
