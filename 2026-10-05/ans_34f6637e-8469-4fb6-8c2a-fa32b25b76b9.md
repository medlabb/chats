## Answer

 **Neonatal jaundice**, when associated with **hyperbilirubinemia**, may cause **green discoloration of developing teeth**. Biliary atresia with resultant hyperbilirubinemia often causes green teeth, and staining may affect both the primary and some permanent dentitions when exposure occurs during early development (1).
