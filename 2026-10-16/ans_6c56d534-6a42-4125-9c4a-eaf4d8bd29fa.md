```
which type of biopsy to take from parotid lesion?
```

- - -

## Answer

 **Ultrasound-guided fine-needle aspiration (FNA)** is the usual initial biopsy for a parotid lesion. CT guidance may be used for deep-lobe tumors that are not palpable or well visualized on ultrasound. (1)

**Core needle biopsy (CNB)** is generally reserved for a nondiagnostic FNA, or when histologic architecture is needed—for example, suspected lymphoma/systemic disease, a patient unable to tolerate surgery, or when histology is important for operative planning. (1) (2)

**Open/incisional biopsy** should generally be avoided for a suspected primary salivary neoplasm or malignancy; it is considered mainly after FNA/CNB when additional tissue is needed, such as for lymphoma or an inflammatory/systemic process. (3)
