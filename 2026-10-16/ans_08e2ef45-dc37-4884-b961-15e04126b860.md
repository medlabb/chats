```
How much feed does a newborn need (if needing top up)?
```

- - -

## Answer

 For a **healthy term newborn needing a top-up**, suggested supplement volumes are:

- **First 24 hours:** 2–10 mL per feed  
- **24–48 hours:** 5–15 mL per feed  
- **48–72 hours:** 15–30 mL per feed  
- **72–96 hours:** 30–60 mL per feed  

These should be given **alongside breastfeeding**, not routinely instead of it. If using a supplement other than freshly expressed breast milk, express or pump after each supplemented feed to support milk production. (1)

For healthy formula-fed newborns, feeds are generally offered on demand, no more than four hours apart; the volume is typically **15–30 mL per feed during the first few days**. (2)
