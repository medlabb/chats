```
why fibrin degeneration products (FDP) are elevated in ALF
```

- - -

## Answer

 **Fibrin degradation products (FDPs) are elevated in acute liver failure because fibrinolysis is enhanced.** Acute liver failure can cause **accelerated intravascular coagulation and fibrinolysis (AICF)**, with increased plasmin-mediated breakdown of intravascular fibrin into FDPs. Disseminated intravascular coagulation (DIC), when present, can further increase fibrin formation and subsequent degradation. (1)

Plasmin is the key fibrinolytic enzyme that digests fibrin into FDPs; degradation of cross-linked fibrin also releases **D-dimers**. (rf_87)
