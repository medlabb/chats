## Answer

 **SLE classification criteria are not diagnostic criteria**; diagnosis requires individualized clinical judgment and exclusion of other diseases. (1)

### 2019 EULAR/ACR classification criteria
- **Entry criterion:** positive ANA at a titer of **≥1:80**. Without this, the patient does not meet the EULAR/ACR classification criteria. (1)
- Clinical and immunologic findings are then assigned **weighted points across several domains**.
- A total of **≥10 points** is required for classification as SLE. (1)
- No single clinical or laboratory finding provides 10 points, **except a renal biopsy showing class III or IV lupus nephritis**. (1)
- Anti-dsDNA and anti-Sm are the ANA subtypes included in the current EULAR/ACR criteria. (2)

The **2012 SLICC criteria** are also used for clinical-trial enrollment, but the specific item-by-item requirements are not provided in the selected references. (1)
