```
why fibrin degeneration products (FDP) are elevated in ALF
```

- - -

## Answer

 **Fibrin degradation products (FDPs)** may be elevated in acute liver failure (ALF) because:

- **Increased fibrinolysis:** Plasmin breaks down fibrin and fibrinogen, producing FDPs. Liver disease may involve increased tissue plasminogen activator (tPA), reduced antifibrinolytic factors, and an AICF-like process, promoting fibrin breakdown. (1) (rf_94)
- **Reduced hepatic clearance:** Hepatocytes and Kupffer cells normally clear coagulation factors and fibrinolytic products; impaired liver function can allow FDPs to accumulate in the circulation. (rf_94)
- **Possible concurrent DIC:** FDPs are elevated in DIC, and liver disease and DIC may coexist, particularly with infection or sepsis. (2) (3)

FDP elevation is therefore **not specific for DIC** and should be interpreted with the clinical context and other coagulation findings. (2) (3)
