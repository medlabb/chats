## Hepatocellular injury
Hepatocellular injury is typically characterized by the release of intracellular proteins and small molecules into the plasma. Thus, in contrast to cholestatic syndromes, the elevations in serum conjugated and unconjugated bilirubin and bile salts are accompanied by elevations in the serum concentrations of hepatocellular enzymes, such as AST and ALT.
