```
in Acute liver failure, what is the type of jaundice?
```

- - -

## Answer

 In **acute liver failure**, the jaundice is typically **hepatocellular jaundice**, with elevations of both **conjugated and unconjugated bilirubin** due to hepatocellular injury (1).
