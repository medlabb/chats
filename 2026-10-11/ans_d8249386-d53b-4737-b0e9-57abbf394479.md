```
When to treat anemia in ICU patients with IV iron? Which patients are eligible?
```

- - -

## Answer

 **IV (parenteral) iron may be considered for ICU patients with confirmed iron-deficiency anemia**, particularly when anemia is severe or oral iron is not suitable; the reference describes parenteral iron as appropriate only in “special cases.” Iron deficiency is supported by microcytic red cells with reduced hemoglobin content and by low serum or bone-marrow iron stores. (1) (2)

**Do not give iron for hemolytic anemia**, because iron stores are elevated rather than depleted. (1)

The selected references do **not** provide ICU-specific timing, hemoglobin thresholds, or detailed eligibility criteria beyond confirmed iron deficiency and special/severe cases.
