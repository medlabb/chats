## Answer

 There is **no formula or fixed needle depth provided** for a 2‑month-old weighing 5 kg. The required depth varies with the infant’s **size and body habitus**, so weight alone cannot determine how many centimeters to insert. Advance slowly and intermittently check for CSF; ultrasound can help identify the safest site and depth in infants. (1) (2)

For an infant under 12 months:

- Perform the LP below the **L2–L3 interspace**, typically at **L3–L4 or L4–L5**. (3)  
- In the lateral position, the needle is directed approximately **45° from perpendicular**, aiming cephalad toward the umbilicus. (1)  
- Use ultrasound when available to determine the appropriate insertion depth rather than relying on a weight-based formula. (2)
