## Answer

 **Rheumatoid arthritis (RA) can rarely be associated with dementia**, usually as a manifestation of **central nervous system (CNS) vasculitis**. Reported symptoms of RA-associated CNS vasculitis include mental-status changes, seizures, paralysis, aphasia, gait disorders, and dementia. This condition is rare and is more often reported in people with longstanding, erosive, seropositive RA. (1)

Another rare complication, **rheumatoid meningitis**, may cause altered mental status, headaches, seizures, and other neurological symptoms. MRI typically shows meningeal thickening or enhancement, and other causes such as infection must be excluded. (2)

The references do not establish that typical RA commonly causes dementia.
