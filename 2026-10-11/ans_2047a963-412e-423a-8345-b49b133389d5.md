```
is ACE inhibitors contraindicated in renal artery stenosis
```

- - -

## Answer

 **ACE inhibitors are not absolutely contraindicated in renal artery stenosis**, but they require caution and monitoring.

- **Unilateral renal artery stenosis:** ACE inhibitors may reduce GFR in the affected kidney, but total GFR is usually maintained by the opposite kidney. They may be used as first-line antihypertensive therapy. (1) (2)
- **Bilateral renal artery stenosis or stenosis affecting a solitary functioning kidney:** They can cause a significant fall in GFR and acute kidney injury by impairing the compensatory angiotensin II response. They may still be used in selected patients with careful monitoring. (3)
- Check serum creatinine shortly after starting treatment. A rise of **more than 30%** from baseline is concerning and may require stopping the ACE inhibitor, using another antihypertensive, or considering correction of the stenosis. (3) (4)
