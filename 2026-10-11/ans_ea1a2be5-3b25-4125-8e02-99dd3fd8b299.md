## Answer

 **Pneumonia** is an infection of the lungs that can cause **cough, fever, and difficulty breathing**. It may be caused by bacteria, viruses, or other germs. (1)

Common symptoms include **chills, shortness of breath, chest pain when breathing, rapid heart or breathing rate, nausea, vomiting, diarrhea, and cough**, sometimes producing green or yellow sputum. Confusion may also occur, particularly in serious illness. (2)

Pneumonia can affect anyone but is especially serious in **young children, adults over 65, and people with underlying health problems or weakened immunity**. (3) It is usually diagnosed using a medical history, physical examination, and chest X-ray. (4)
