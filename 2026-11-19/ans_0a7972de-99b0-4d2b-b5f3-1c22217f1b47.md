## Answer

 **Familial Mediterranean fever (FMF) colchicine dosing:**

- **Children <5 years:** ≤0.5 mg once daily (≤0.6 mg/day if using 0.6-mg tablets).
- **Children 5–10 years:** 0.5–1 mg/day.
- **Children >10 years and adults:** 1–1.5 mg/day, preferably once daily; divide the dose only if not tolerated. (1)
- **Higher initial doses:** Up to 2 mg/day may be considered for greater disease activity or renal amyloidosis **when kidney and liver function are normal**. (1)
- **Titration:** Increase by 0.5–0.6 mg increments according to response and tolerance.
- **Maximum:** 2 mg/day in children under 12 years and 3 mg/day in adults. (1)

Another dosing reference lists **1–1.5 mg/day initially** and titration by 0.5–0.6 mg increments, with a **maximum of 3 mg/day**. (2)

**Renal or hepatic impairment requires dose adjustment and close monitoring**; for severe renal impairment, the pediatric/adolescent reference lists an initial dose of **0.3 mg/day**. (3) Colchicine should not be increased during an acute FMF attack, and doses above 3 mg/day are associated with significant adverse effects. (1)
