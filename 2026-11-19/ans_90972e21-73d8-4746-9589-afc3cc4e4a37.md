 ## Classification of Hepatorenal Syndrome (HRS)

- **HRS-AKI (acute kidney injury):** Acute, severe cirrhosis-mediated kidney dysfunction; this is the current primary focus and corresponds to most cases previously classified as type 1 HRS.
- **HRS-nAKI (nonacute kidney injury):** A proposed term for cirrhosis-associated kidney dysfunction that is subacute, chronic, or less severe than HRS-AKI.
- **HRS-AKD (acute kidney disease):** Kidney dysfunction lasting **7–90 days**.
- **HRS-CKD (chronic kidney disease):** Kidney dysfunction lasting **more than 90 days**.

The former **type 1 and type 2 HRS** classification is no longer used. (1)
