```
is EUS indicated if CT shows a pancreatic mass?
```

## Answer

 **EUS with tissue sampling is generally indicated or strongly considered when CT shows a pancreatic mass**, because it can:

- Obtain histologic confirmation and help distinguish adenocarcinoma from lymphoma, neuroendocrine tumor, metastasis, autoimmune pancreatitis, or chronic pancreatitis.
- Further evaluate a lesion that is indeterminate on CT.
- Stage disease by assessing vascular relationships, regional lymph nodes, and selected metastases.
- Provide tissue when percutaneous biopsy is unsafe, such as when the lesion is surrounded by vessels. (1) (2)

For solid pancreatic lesions, **EUS-guided fine-needle biopsy (FNB)** can be used; it may be particularly useful when FNA is nondiagnostic or additional tissue is needed. (1)

However, the selected references do not establish that EUS is mandatory for every CT-detected mass. In patients with clear radiologic indications for surgery—specifically discussed for certain IPMNs—EUS-FNA may not be needed, whereas invasive-appearing lesions generally require additional evaluation to confirm diagnosis and stage malignancy. (3)
