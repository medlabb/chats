```
If a neonate has dvt and took clexan then after 2 weeks the doppler showed no dvt 
Shall we stop clexan or continue 4-6 weeks ?
```

- - -

## Answer

 A **normal Doppler after 2 weeks does not, by itself, establish that Clexane (enoxaparin) should be stopped**. For acute symptomatic neonatal central-line–related DVT, the referenced recommendation is anticoagulation for a **total of 6–12 weeks**. (1)

A 6-week course may be adequate for some **provoked venous thromboembolisms**, but neonatal data are limited, so treatment duration should be individualized according to the original DVT site and extent, provoking factors, bleeding risk, and follow-up imaging. (2)

Therefore, **do not stop Clexane solely because the 2-week Doppler is clear**; the neonatology/hematology team should decide the total duration. If a central line remains necessary and is still present after therapeutic anticoagulation, the reference recommends prophylactic anticoagulation until the line is removed. (1)
