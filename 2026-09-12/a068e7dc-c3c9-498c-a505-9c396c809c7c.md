Which of the following is the most common cause of apnea in adults?
​A: Central nervous system depression

​C: Neuromuscular disease
​D: Chest wall deformity

## Answer

 **B: Obstructive sleep apnea** — OSA is more common than central sleep apnea and is the most common sleep-related breathing disorder. (1) (2)
