## Answer

 **Aspirin dose depends on the reason for use:**

- **Primary prevention:** 75–100 mg once daily. (1)  
- **Long-term secondary prevention of cardiovascular disease:** commonly 75–100 mg once daily; 325 mg may be used in selected patients. (2)  
- **Acute occlusive cardiovascular event:** loading dose at least 162 mg, preferably 325 mg. (3)  
- **Stable ischemic heart disease:** 75–100 mg once daily. (4)  
- **Certain stroke/TIA protocols:** initial 160–325 mg, followed by a maintenance dose that may range from 50–325 mg daily depending on the regimen. (4)

The appropriate dose depends on your diagnosis and bleeding risk; use the dose prescribed by your clinician.
