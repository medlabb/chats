## Answer

 **Aspirin dose depends on the indication:**

- **Long-term secondary cardiovascular prevention:** commonly **75–100 mg once daily**; doses up to **325 mg/day** have been studied. Bleeding risk increases above 325 mg/day. (1)
- **Acute occlusive cardiovascular event:** loading dose **at least 162 mg**, preferably **325 mg** of regular aspirin. (2)
- **Children:** dosing varies substantially by condition and weight; for example, Kawasaki disease uses **30–50 mg/kg/day** during the acute phase, followed by **3–5 mg/kg/day**. (3)
