## Answer

 **Aspirin dose depends on the indication:**

- **Primary cardiovascular prevention:** 75–100 mg once daily (81 mg is a common low-dose tablet). (1)
- **Long-term secondary prevention after cardiovascular disease:** commonly 75–100 mg/day; 325 mg/day may be used in selected patients. (2)
- **Acute occlusive cardiovascular event:** at least 162 mg of regular aspirin, preferably 325 mg, as a loading dose. (3)

The appropriate dose for pain, children, or another specific condition cannot be found in the provided references.
