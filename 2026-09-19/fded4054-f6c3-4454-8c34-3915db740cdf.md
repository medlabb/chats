## Answer

 The provided references support the following **newborn/term infant** ranges:

| Age group | Normal respiratory rate | Normal heart rate |
|---|---:|---:|
| **Healthy infants/term newborns** | Approximately **40–60 breaths/min** (1) | **120–160 beats/min** (2) |
| **During sleep** | — | May decrease to approximately **85 beats/min** (2) |
| **First hour after birth** | May be higher; about **50% of healthy newborns** have rates above 60 breaths/min (1) | — |

A respiratory rate **greater than 60 breaths/min**, especially with retractions, nasal flaring, or grunting, is considered a sign of respiratory distress (3).

Normal vital-sign ranges for older children and adults cannot be found in the provided references.
