## Answer

 **Aspirin dose depends on the indication:**

- **Primary prevention:** 75–100 mg once daily; 81 mg is a commonly available low-dose tablet. (1)
- **Long-term secondary cardiovascular prevention:** Most experts use 75–100 mg once daily; 325 mg may be used in selected patients. (2)
- **Acute occlusive cardiovascular event:** Loading dose of at least 162 mg of regular aspirin, preferably 325 mg. (rf_f8)

The appropriate dose depends on your condition and bleeding risk; follow the prescribing clinician’s instructions.
