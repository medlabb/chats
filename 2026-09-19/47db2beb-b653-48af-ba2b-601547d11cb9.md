## Answer

 **Aspirin** irreversibly inhibits COX-1 and COX-2, reducing prostaglandin formation. It also inhibits platelet aggregation by reducing thromboxane A₂ formation and has antipyretic, analgesic, and anti-inflammatory effects. (1)

Its platelet-inhibitory effect may last for the platelet’s lifetime—approximately 10 days. (1)

The provided references also describe **salicylism and overdose toxicity**, including nausea, vomiting, hyperventilation, confusion, pulmonary edema, seizures, coma, cardiovascular collapse, and renal or respiratory failure at higher concentrations. (1)
