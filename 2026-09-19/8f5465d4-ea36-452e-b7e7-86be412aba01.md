## Answer

 If you mean **aspirin (Asporin) for preeclampsia prevention in pregnancy**, the recommended dose is **81 mg once daily**. It is generally started between **12 and 28 weeks’ gestation—ideally before 16 weeks—and continued until delivery**. (1)

For **multifetal pregnancy**, 81 mg daily is recommended from **12–16 weeks until delivery**. (2)

The appropriate dose for other conditions cannot be found in the provided references.
