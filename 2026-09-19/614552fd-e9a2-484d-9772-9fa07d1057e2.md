## Answer

 **Hepatitis** is inflammation of the liver. It can result from viruses such as hepatitis A, B, or C, alcohol use, certain medications or herbal products, and poisons. (1)

**Hepatitis A** is a viral form of hepatitis that occurs worldwide. (2)
