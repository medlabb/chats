## Answer

 **Aspirin dose depends on the indication:**

- **Primary cardiovascular prevention:** 75–100 mg once daily; 81 mg is a common low-dose tablet. (rf_47)
- **Long-term secondary prevention after cardiovascular disease:** commonly 75–100 mg/day; 325 mg/day may be used in selected patients. (1)
- **Acute occlusive cardiovascular event:** loading dose of at least 162 mg of regular aspirin, preferably 325 mg. (rf_88)

The appropriate dose depends on your condition, bleeding risk, age, and other medications—confirm it with a clinician.
