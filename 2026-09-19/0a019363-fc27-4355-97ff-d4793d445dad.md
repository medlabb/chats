## Answer

 **Aspirin dose depends on why it is being taken:**

- **Pain or fever:** 325 mg–1 g by mouth every 4–6 hours as needed; usual maximum is **4 g/day**. (1)
- **Cardiovascular secondary prevention:** typically **75–100 mg once daily**. (2)
- **Suspected acute coronary syndrome:** **162–325 mg once**, preferably a non-enteric-coated tablet that is chewed and swallowed—seek emergency care immediately. (2)
- **Primary prevention:** when specifically chosen with a healthcare professional, **75–100 mg daily**. (3)

The appropriate dose depends on your **condition, age, bleeding risk, pregnancy status, kidney function, and other medicines**. What are you taking aspirin for?
