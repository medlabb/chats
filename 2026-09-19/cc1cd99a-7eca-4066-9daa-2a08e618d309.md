## Answer

 **Aspirin dose depends on the reason for use:**

- **Pain or fever (adults):** 325–1,000 mg by mouth every 4–6 hours as needed; usual maximum **4,000 mg/day**. (1)
- **Cardiovascular secondary prevention:** 75–100 mg once daily. (2)
- **Suspected acute coronary syndrome:** 162–325 mg once, using immediate-release, non-enteric-coated aspirin; chew and swallow. (2)
- **Children:** Do **not** give aspirin to anyone under 18 with or recovering from chickenpox or flu-like illness because of the association with Reye syndrome. (3)

The appropriate dose cannot be determined without knowing the **indication, age, formulation, medical conditions, and other medications**.
