## Answer

 **Reticulocyte count** measures the percentage or absolute number of newly produced red blood cells (RBCs), reflecting bone-marrow RBC production. (rf_70)

- **Normal percentage:** approximately **1–2%** of circulating RBCs; another reference gives **0.5–1.5%**. (1) (2)  
- **Absolute count at steady state:** approximately **25,000–100,000/µL**. (rf_70)  
- **Increased count:** usually indicates an appropriate marrow response to **hemolysis, blood loss, or recovery after treatment of nutritional deficiency**. (3)  
- **Low or inappropriately normal count:** suggests impaired RBC production, such as **bone-marrow suppression or iron, vitamin B12, folate, or copper deficiency**. (3)  
- In anemia, the percentage should be interpreted in relation to the hematocrit; a corrected reticulocyte count can be calculated as:  
  **Reticulocyte % × patient HCT ÷ normal HCT**. (4)
