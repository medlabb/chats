```
Do we stop linezolid in case of bleeding?
```

- - -

## Answer

 **Do not automatically stop linezolid solely because bleeding occurs** based on the selected references. However, bleeding warrants prompt evaluation with a **complete blood count and platelet count**, because linezolid can cause myelosuppression—most commonly thrombocytopenia—and CBC monitoring is specifically recommended in patients at increased risk of bleeding. (1) (2)

If linezolid-associated myelosuppression is identified, it is typically reversible after discontinuation, with recovery generally occurring within **1–3 weeks**. (3) (4)

The selected references do not specify a definitive bleeding threshold or a universal instruction to discontinue linezolid in every bleeding episode.
