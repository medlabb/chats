## Answer

 ## Allergic rhinitis

**Allergic rhinitis** is inflammation of the nasal passages caused by a reaction to airborne allergens. It commonly causes:

- Sneezing  
- Itchy nose, eyes, or palate  
- Runny or watery nasal discharge  
- Nasal congestion  
- Postnasal drainage  

(1) (2)

Common triggers include **pollens, outdoor molds, house-dust mites, cockroaches, animal allergens, rodents, and indoor fungi**. Symptoms may be **intermittent**, **seasonal**, or **persistent year-round**. (3)

The nasal lining may appear **pale and swollen**. Associated problems can include allergic conjunctivitis, poor sleep, fatigue, rhinosinusitis, and asthma. (3)

Diagnosis is generally based on the symptoms and physical examination; testing can confirm the diagnosis and identify specific allergens. (4) (2)

Treatment may include **reducing allergen exposure**, medications, nasal irrigation, allergy shots, or tablets dissolved under the tongue. The appropriate treatment depends on symptom severity and individual preferences. (5)
