 ## Allergic rhinitis

**Allergic rhinitis** is an IgE-mediated rhinitis characterized by sneezing, rhinorrhea, nasal obstruction, itching of the conjunctiva, nose and pharynx, and lacrimation. It may be **seasonal**, commonly triggered by pollens, or **perennial**, due to ongoing exposure to dust mites, animal dander, molds, or cockroach allergens. (1)

### Risk factors and associations
It commonly occurs in atopic individuals and may coexist with atopic dermatitis, food allergy, urticaria, and asthma. Female sex, particulate air pollution, and maternal tobacco smoking increase risk. (2)

### Typical findings
Hallmark manifestations include episodic rhinorrhea, sneezing, nasal obstruction, lacrimation, and itching. Examination may show a pale, boggy nasal mucosa and congested, edematous conjunctivae. Turbinate and mucosal swelling can contribute to sinus or middle-ear complications. (3)

### Diagnosis
Diagnosis requires:

- A compatible history, often temporally related to allergen exposure
- Demonstration of sensitization to an environmental allergen (1)

Skin-prick testing is a rapid and reliable method for identifying allergen-specific IgE. Serum-specific IgE testing is less sensitive than skin testing but has high specificity. (4) (5)
