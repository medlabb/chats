## Answer

 ## **Dementia with Lewy bodies (Lewy body dementia)**

Lewy body dementia is a neurodegenerative disorder associated with **α-synuclein** and Lewy bodies in the brain. Dementia begins **before or within 1 year of parkinsonian symptoms**; if dementia develops more than 1 year after established Parkinson disease, it is termed Parkinson disease with dementia. (1)

Typical features include:

- **Fluctuating cognition**
- **Well-formed visual hallucinations**
- Parkinsonism, particularly **rigidity and bradykinesia**
- **REM sleep behavior disorder** with dream enactment
- Autonomic symptoms such as **postural hypotension** and syncope
- Cognitive decline that may occur without prominent early memory impairment (2) (1)

Treatment is challenging. **Cholinesterase inhibitors** may improve dementia and behavioral symptoms; memantine may also be beneficial. Levodopa may provide limited motor benefit but can worsen hallucinations and cognitive or behavioral disturbances. Anticholinergic drugs should generally be avoided, and antipsychotics are often poorly tolerated. Caregiver education and support are important. (2) (3)
