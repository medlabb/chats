## Answer

 **B. Bradyarrhythmia**

The most likely cause is intermittent **high-grade conduction disease causing bradyarrhythmia**, such as advanced AV block or a prolonged pause. Bradyarrhythmias can cause abrupt syncope without warning, and baseline conduction abnormalities with syncope may indicate a conduction-system disorder. (1) (2)

The occasional premature ventricular beats are less likely because ventricular premature beats rarely cause complete syncope unless associated with ventricular bigeminy, marked bradycardia, or significant ventricular dysfunction. Torsades de pointes is unlikely with a normal QTc. (1)
