**Aspirin dose depends on the indication:**

- **Primary prevention of cardiovascular disease:** **75–100 mg once daily** (commonly 81 mg). (1) (2)
- **Secondary prevention of cardiovascular disease/ASCVD:** usually **75–100 mg once daily**; doses up to 325 mg/day have been studied. (3)
- **Acute coronary syndrome or acute occlusive cardiovascular event:** **162–325 mg once**, preferably chewed for rapid effect, followed by **75–100 mg once daily** for maintenance. (4) (5)
- **Secondary prevention after ischemic stroke/TIA:** initial **160–325 mg once**, then generally **50–325 mg once daily**; some experts use **50–100 mg daily**. (rf_36)
- **Stable ischemic heart disease:** **75–100 mg once daily**. (rf_36)

The appropriate dose depends on the specific condition, bleeding risk, and other medications.
