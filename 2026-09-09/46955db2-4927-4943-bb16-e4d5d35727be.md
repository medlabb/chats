**Aspirin dose depends on the indication:**

- **Primary prevention of cardiovascular disease:** 75–100 mg orally once daily (commonly 81 mg). (1) (2)
- **Secondary prevention of cardiovascular disease:** typically 75–100 mg once daily; 325 mg daily may be used in selected patients. (3)
- **Suspected acute coronary syndrome or acute myocardial infarction:** 162–325 mg of immediate-release aspirin, chewed once initially; maintenance is usually 75–100 mg once daily. (4) (5)
- **Secondary prevention after ischemic stroke/TIA:** maintenance dosing may range from 50–325 mg once daily, with some experts favoring 50–100 mg daily. (6)

The appropriate dose depends on the specific condition, bleeding risk, and other medications.
