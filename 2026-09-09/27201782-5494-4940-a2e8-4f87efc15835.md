For **transient arterial obstruction (vasospasm) after arterial cannulation in a neonate**:

- **Warm the arterial site** with a hot pack.
- If needed, administer **intravenous lidocaine 0.5 mg/kg**.
- If these measures fail, apply a **thin film of 2% topical nitroglycerin ointment** to the wrist, palm, and fingers at **4 mm/kg**. In four neonates with radial artery catheters, this was associated with rapid improvement in hand color and perfusion. Transient blood-pressure reduction was reported, but no serious hypotension occurred. (1)
