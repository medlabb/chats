**C. Rapidly assess cardiac function and volume-related findings to guide immediate management.**

POCUS has an increasing role in evaluating shock because it is rapid, noninvasive, and can assess cardiac function, intravascular volume status, and other causes of hemodynamic instability. Severely reduced LV contractility is a finding that can guide immediate management. POCUS is complementary to formal echocardiography and does **not** replace it. (1) (2)
