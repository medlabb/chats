Following arterial line placement, **blanching or cyanosis of the distal extremity or buttock** may indicate vasospasm, although thrombotic or embolic events are also possible. (1)

The selected reference notes that risk may be reduced by:

- **High umbilical arterial catheter placement** at T6–T8 rather than L3–L4. (1)
- Adding a **small amount of heparin** to the continuous infusion to reduce arterial thrombi. (1)
- Performing flushing with a **small volume over several seconds**. (1)
- Limiting catheter dwell time and using careful insertion and removal technique. (1)

Specific treatment or step-by-step management of established vascular spasm cannot be found in the selected references.
