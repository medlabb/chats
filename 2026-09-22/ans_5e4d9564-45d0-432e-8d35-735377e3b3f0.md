## Answer

 **Neonatal jaundice** is jaundice caused by accumulation of bilirubin in body tissues. The references describe two broad categories:

- **Unconjugated (indirect) hyperbilirubinemia:** may result from increased bilirubin production, such as hemolysis, or impaired hepatic uptake or conjugation. Hemolytic disease related to maternal **Rh(D) antibodies** is one potential cause. (1) (2)
- **Conjugated (direct) hyperbilirubinemia:** may result from hepatocellular injury, intrahepatic cholestasis, or biliary obstruction; **biliary atresia** is listed among the obstructive causes. (3)

For prevention of Rh(D)-mediated hemolytic disease, Rh(D)-negative mothers are given Rh(D) immune globulin routinely at 28 weeks’ gestation and within 72 hours after delivery; it is not helpful once the mother is already alloimmunized. (2)
