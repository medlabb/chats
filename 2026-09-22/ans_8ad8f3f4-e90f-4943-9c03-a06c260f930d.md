## Answer

 **Propranolol dosing depends on the indication:**

- **Essential tremor:** 40–160 mg orally **twice daily**. For predictable, occasional triggers, a single oral dose of **40–120 mg** may be taken beforehand. (1)
- **Migraine prevention:**  
  - Immediate-release: **20–120 mg twice daily**  
  - Long-acting: **60–320 mg/day** (Table 6-8). (2)

Adverse effects include bradycardia, fatigue, depression, and bronchospasm. The reference lists propranolol as contraindicated in **asthma, congestive heart failure, and use with calcium-channel blockers**. (1) (2)
