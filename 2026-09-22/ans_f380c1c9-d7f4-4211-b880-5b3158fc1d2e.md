Feeding refusal when baby is teething

Any tip

## Answer

 Teething may cause **irritability, drooling, and gum redness**, and some infants may temporarily feed less. Tips supported by the references include:

- Offer an **iced teething ring**.
- An **oral analgesic** may be used for symptoms—follow your pediatrician’s dosing advice.
- **Avoid benzocaine-containing teething products**, “natural” or homeopathic remedies, and teething necklaces or bracelets because of potential serious risks. (1)

The selected references do not provide further specific guidance for feeding refusal during ordinary teething. If the refusal is associated with a **loose natal or neonatal tooth**, feeding refusal can occur and requires individual medical assessment because of aspiration risk. (2)
