## Answer

 Teething discomfort can be managed with a **chilled—not frozen—one-piece teething ring or other teething device**. Avoid dipping it in sugary substances, and avoid teething necklaces/bracelets because of choking and strangulation risks. Over-the-counter or prescription topical gels, including lidocaine or benzocaine, are not recommended. (1) (2)

If your baby has **severe symptoms or a fever above 38°C (100.4°F)**, these should not automatically be attributed to teething and should be medically evaluated. (2)
