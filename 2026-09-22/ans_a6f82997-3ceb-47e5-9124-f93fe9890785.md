## Answer

 **Harlequin color change** is a transient neonatal phenomenon, often seen in low-birth-weight infants. The dependent side of the body becomes intensely red while the upper side pales, separated by a sharp midline. Episodes typically last a few minutes and may occur during the first 3 weeks of life. It is attributed to temporary autonomic imbalance in the skin’s blood vessels, with no associated vital-sign changes or long-term consequences reported. (1; 2)
