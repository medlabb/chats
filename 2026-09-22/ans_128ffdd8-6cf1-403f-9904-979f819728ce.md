## Answer

 **Pediatric immune thrombocytopenia (ITP)** is an immune-mediated condition causing an isolated platelet count below **100,000/µL**, with otherwise normal white blood cell count, hemoglobin, and typically peripheral smear findings. It may follow a viral infection, and many children improve spontaneously, often within three months. (1) (2)
