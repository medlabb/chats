## Answer

 **Aspirin dose depends on the indication:**

- **Primary cardiovascular prevention:** 75–100 mg once daily. (1)
- **Secondary cardiovascular prevention:** commonly 75–100 mg once daily. (2)
- **Acute coronary syndrome:** 162–325 mg once, chewed and swallowed, followed by 75–100 mg once daily. (3)
- **Pain or fever:** 325 mg–1 g every 4–6 hours as needed; usual maximum 4 g/day. (4)

The appropriate dose for your specific situation cannot be determined from the question alone.
