```
A 4-year-old girl has fever for six days, bilateral red eyes without discharge, cracked red lips, a widespread rash and swollen hands and feet. 

A unilateral cervical lymph node measures 2 cm.

There is no cough or coryza.

What is the most likely diagnosis?

A. Scarlet fever
B. Measles
C. Kawasaki disease
D. Adenovirus infection
```

- - -

## Answer

 **C. Kawasaki disease.** The presentation fulfills the diagnostic criteria: fever for ≥5 days plus bilateral nonexudative conjunctivitis, fissured lips, swollen hands and feet, polymorphous rash, and cervical lymphadenopathy >1.5 cm (1).
