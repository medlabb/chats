### Interpretation of persistent fever despite two negative blood cultures

Two negative blood cultures do not establish the cause of the fever. The child still requires repeated clinical assessment and a search for an occult focus. Important possible sources include:

- **Pneumonia**, given cough, tachypnea, oxygen requirement, and respiratory deterioration
