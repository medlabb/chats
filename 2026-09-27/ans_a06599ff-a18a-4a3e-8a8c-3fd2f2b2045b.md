## Answer

 **Aspirin dose depends on the indication:**

- **Primary cardiovascular prevention:** 75–100 mg once daily (1; 2).
- **Secondary cardiovascular prevention/maintenance:** commonly 75–100 mg once daily; doses of 75–325 mg/day have been studied (3; 4).
- **Acute coronary syndrome or acute occlusive cardiovascular event:** 162–325 mg once, preferably chewed for rapid effect (4; 5).
- **Pain or fever:** 325 mg–1 g every 4–6 hours as needed; usual maximum 4 g/day (6).

The appropriate dose cannot be determined without knowing the reason for taking aspirin.
