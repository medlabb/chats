## Answer

 **Dermatology** includes the evaluation of skin, scalp, nails, and mucosal surfaces through history-taking and physical examination, focusing on lesion morphology, arrangement, and distribution. Additional tools may include **Wood’s lamp examination** and **dermoscopy**. (1)

Common dermatologic conditions described include **seborrheic dermatitis, atopic dermatitis, allergic and irritant contact dermatitis, stasis dermatitis, asteatotic eczema,** and **dyshidrotic eczema**. (2)

**Dermoscopy** is a noninvasive surface-microscopy technique used to assess features such as pigment distribution, vascular morphology, lesion borders, scaling, and subsurface skin structures. (3) (4)
