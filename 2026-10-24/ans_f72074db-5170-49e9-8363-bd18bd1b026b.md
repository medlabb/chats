 ## **Diabetes diagnosis**

Diabetes mellitus is diagnosed using abnormal glycemia testing, generally confirmed on **two different occasions when symptoms are absent**. (1)

| Test | Prediabetes | Diabetes |
|---|---:|---:|
| **Fasting plasma glucose** | 100–125 mg/dL (5.6–6.9 mmol/L) | **≥126 mg/dL (7.0 mmol/L)** |
| **2-hour glucose after 75-g oral glucose tolerance test** | 140–199 mg/dL (7.8–11.0 mmol/L) | **≥200 mg/dL (11.1 mmol/L)** |
| **HbA1c** | 5.7–6.4% | **≥6.5%** |

These criteria are summarized in **Table 32–4**. A fasting glucose ≥126 mg/dL should be confirmed by repeat testing; fasting glucose ≥126 mg/dL and HbA1c ≥6.5% from the same sample also meet diagnostic criteria. (1)

For a standardized oral glucose tolerance test, the patient consumes **75 g glucose**, with blood glucose measured at fasting and 2 hours; a 2-hour value ≥200 mg/dL is diagnostic. (2)

Type 1 diabetes may present with **polyuria, polydipsia, weight loss, glucose ≥200 mg/dL, ketones, and islet autoantibodies**. Type 2 diabetes is often associated with obesity and may be asymptomatic at diagnosis. (3)

## **Treatment**

Treatment should be individualized according to the diabetes type and the patient’s needs. Core management includes:

- **Diabetes self-management education and support**
- **Medical nutrition therapy**
- **Regular physical activity**
- Adequate-quality sleep
- Tobacco/vaping cessation support
- Psychosocial care and shared decision-making (4)

### **Type 1 diabetes**

Treatment requires **insulin replacement**. Rapid-acting insulin is combined with longer-acting basal insulin; intensive regimens commonly use multiple daily injections. (5)

### **Type 2 diabetes**

Pharmacologic treatment is recommended **at diagnosis**, together with weight management and exercise. **Metformin** remains an effective, lower-cost first-line option when cardiovascular or kidney disease is not present. For patients with cardiovascular or kidney disease, a **GLP-1 receptor agonist** or **SGLT-2 inhibitor** may be selected; GLP-1 or dual GIP/GLP-1 therapy may be useful when weight loss is a priority. **Figure 32–2** presents the treatment algorithm.
