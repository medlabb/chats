```
For a neonate with aki and on tpn we shall give aminovien at which dose
```

## Answer

 The selected references **do not specify an Aminoven dose specifically for neonates with AKI**.

For general neonatal parenteral amino-acid therapy, the references describe:

- **1–1.5 g/kg/day** as sufficient to limit catabolism.
- **2.5–3 g/kg/day** as an early parenteral amino-acid dose in extremely premature infants.
- A maximum of **3.5 g/kg/day parenterally** is cited for very-low-birth-weight infants. (1) (2) (3)

In AKI, fluid should be restricted to urine output plus insensible losses, and concentrated parenteral nutrition may be required; however, an AKI-specific amino-acid dose or Aminoven formulation/concentration cannot be found in the selected references. (4)
