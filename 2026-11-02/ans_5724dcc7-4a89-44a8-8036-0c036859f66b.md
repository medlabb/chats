 ## **Staphylococcus aureus**

- **Pneumonia:** *S. aureus* is an important cause of secondary bacterial pneumonia in children and healthy adults after viral respiratory illnesses, such as measles and influenza. (1)  
- Staphylococcal pneumonia has a high incidence of complications, particularly **lung abscess** and **empyema**. (1)  
- Pneumonia associated with **right-sided staphylococcal endocarditis** is a serious complication of intravenous substance use. (1)  
- It is also an important cause of **health care–associated** and **hospital-acquired pneumonia**; hospital-acquired infections are usually methicillin-resistant. (2)  
- *S. aureus* may cause **necrotizing pneumonia and lung abscess**, although this is described as uncommon. (2)
