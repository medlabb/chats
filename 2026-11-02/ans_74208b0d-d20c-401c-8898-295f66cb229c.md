## Answer

 ## **Sarcoidosis**

Sarcoidosis is a **multisystem disorder of unknown cause** in which clusters of inflammatory tissue called **granulomas**—typically **noncaseating or non-necrotizing granulomas**—form in affected organs. (1) (2)

- **Lungs:** Involved in approximately 90% of patients; possible symptoms include cough, shortness of breath, and chest pain. Bilateral hilar and mediastinal lymphadenopathy, pulmonary infiltrates, and—over time—airway narrowing or fibrosis may occur. (3) (4) (2)
- **Other organs:** The skin, eyes, lymph nodes, nose, heart, liver, spleen, kidneys, nervous system, joints, and other organs may be affected. (5) (6)
- **Löfgren syndrome:** A minority of patients develop fever, hilar adenopathy, and erythema nodosum or bilateral ankle arthritis. (7)

### Diagnosis and evaluation

Diagnosis generally requires compatible clinical and radiographic findings, exclusion of other conditions—particularly mycobacterial or fungal infection and malignancy—and usually tissue identification of non-necrotizing granulomas. (7) (2)

Initial evaluation may include a history and physical examination, chest radiograph, blood counts, serum chemistries, and electrocardiogram. High-resolution CT is recommended when pulmonary infiltrates are present. After diagnosis, pulmonary function testing and an ophthalmologic examination are recommended. (2)
