```
which electrolyte abnormalties can cause qrs widening
```

- - -

## Answer

 Electrolyte abnormalities supported by the references include:

- **Hyperkalemia**—causes progressive QRS widening, which may eventually form a sine-wave pattern. (1; 2)
- **Severe hypokalemia**—may increase QRS duration. (3)

The selected references do not identify other electrolyte abnormalities as causes of QRS widening.
