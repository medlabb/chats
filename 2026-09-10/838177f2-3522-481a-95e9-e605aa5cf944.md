- **Remove the arterial catheter**, as already done. (1)
- Assess for **acute limb ischemia**: cool or pale/discolored extremity, poor or absent distal pulses, reduced perfusion, or prolonged capillary refill. (2)
- If arterial thrombosis is causing **clinically significant impairment of limb or organ blood flow**, anticoagulation is recommended. The reference suggests **LMWH over UFH** for most neonates requiring anticoagulation, because of more predictable response and less monitoring. (1)
- **Thrombolysis** may be considered when thrombosis is life-threatening or limb/organ viability is threatened; obtain **surgical consultation** in such cases. (1)
- Before anticoagulation, perform baseline coagulation studies, CBC/platelets, renal and liver function tests, and cranial ultrasound. (1)
- Management should be individualized because neonatal data are limited, and pediatric hematology consultation is advised. (3)
