- The stated **tPA dose of 0.01–0.06 mg/kg/hour** is not supported by the selected reference. Reported systemic regimens are **<0.1–0.6 mg/kg/hour**; the cited practice starts at **0.1–0.2 mg/kg/hour**, without a loading dose, with concurrent UFH at **10 units/kg/hour**. (1)
- **UFH at 28 units/kg/hour** is not supported by the selected references. For most neonatal anticoagulation, **LMWH is preferred over UFH** because of a more predictable response and less monitoring. (2)
- **Thrombectomy** is not simply recommended whenever the above treatments fail. It is an alternative for critical cases but is **rarely performed in neonates** because of small vessel size and clinical instability; surgical consultation is advised when limb or organ viability is threatened. (1)
- A catheter-associated arterial thrombus causing clinically significant impaired blood flow warrants anticoagulation, and the associated arterial catheter should be removed. Diagnosis is confirmed by Doppler ultrasonography. (2) (3)
