## Answer

 **Amiprostone** is listed as an international brand name for **lubiprostone**, a locally acting gastrointestinal **chloride-channel activator** that increases intestinal fluid secretion and improves fecal transit. (1)

**Dose and administration**
- Available as oral capsules: **8 mcg and 24 mcg**. (2)
- Take **with food and water**; swallow the capsule whole—do not break or chew it. (2)
- The selected references do **not provide the recommended dosing frequency or indication-specific dose**, so the complete dose cannot be stated from these references.

**Indications**
- Chronic idiopathic constipation in adults.
- Irritable bowel syndrome with constipation in women aged ≥18 years.
- Opioid-induced constipation in adults with chronic noncancer pain, including certain patients with prior cancer-related pain. (2)
- Off-label: irritable bowel syndrome with constipation in males. (2)

**Drug interactions**
- **Levomethadone** and **methadone** may decrease the therapeutic effects of lubiprostone; monitoring is recommended. (2)
- The reference lists no known metabolism/transport effects. (2)

The references also state that use should be avoided in severe diarrhea and is contraindicated with bowel obstruction; caution or dosage adjustment may be needed in moderate-to-severe hepatic impairment. (2)
