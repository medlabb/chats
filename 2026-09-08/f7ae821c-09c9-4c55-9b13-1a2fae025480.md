**A. Familial hypocalciuric hypercalcemia (FHH).**

The combination of **mild, asymptomatic hypercalcemia**, an **inappropriately normal PTH**, and a **calcium/creatinine clearance ratio <0.01** is most consistent with FHH. FHH characteristically causes longstanding mild hypercalcemia, normal or mildly elevated PTH, and low urinary calcium excretion. (1) A ratio below 0.01 strongly favors FHH over primary hyperparathyroidism, although some overlap exists. (2)
