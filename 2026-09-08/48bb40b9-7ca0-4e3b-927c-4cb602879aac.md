For neonatal hypoglycaemia, the selected references state that **10% dextrose at 3.6–4.8 mL/kg/hour corresponds to a GIR of 6–8 mg/kg/min**. If glucose remains low, increase the infusion by **1–2 mg/kg/min every 3–4 hours** while monitoring the response. (1)

For preterm infants weighing 500–1000 g, a starting GIR of **3–6 mg/kg/min** is recommended, increasing to **7–10 mg/kg/min** on full parenteral nutrition as tolerated. (2)

The exact calculation formula for converting fluid rate and dextrose concentration into GIR cannot be found in the selected references.
