```
Albumin infusion after large volume paracentesis
```

- - -

## Answer

 - **If >5 L of ascitic fluid is removed**, administer intravenous albumin to reduce the risk of post-paracentesis circulatory dysfunction. Recommended dosing is **6–8 g of albumin per liter of ascites removed**. (1) (2)
- For removal of **<5 L**, post-paracentesis colloid infusion generally does not appear necessary. (1)
- **25% albumin** is commonly used; it is typically preferred in hypervolemic patients, while **5% albumin** may be used when dehydration is suspected. (2)
- Albumin reduces the risk of hemodynamic deterioration, hyponatremia, and kidney impairment after large-volume paracentesis. (2)
- The optimal dose has not been firmly established. Albumin infusion should be administered with monitoring, since overly rapid administration may cause volume overload; product guidance generally recommends no faster than **1–2 mL/minute** in patients without shock. (2) (3)
