What is allergic asthma

## Answer

 **Allergic asthma** is asthma associated with **atopy**, meaning a genetic tendency to produce allergen-specific IgE after exposure to allergens. About half of asthma cases in people aged 6–59 were attributed to atopy with evidence of allergic sensitization in one survey. Common associated allergens include **house dust mites, indoor fungi, cockroaches, and indoor animals**. (1)

After sensitization, exposure to the specific allergen can activate IgE-bearing mast cells and basophils, leading to airway **bronchospasm, mucus secretion, inflammation, and airway hyperresponsiveness**. (2) (3) Allergens are listed among the triggers of airway narrowing in asthma. (rf_72)
