```
Dose of fluconazole in esophageal moniliasis?
```

- - -

## Answer

 For **esophageal candidiasis (moniliasis)**:

- **Oral fluconazole:** **400 mg on day 1, then 200–400 mg once daily for 14–21 days**. (1)  
- If oral therapy is not tolerated: **intravenous fluconazole 400 mg once daily for 14–21 days**. (2)

Patients who do not improve within **3–5 days** should undergo endoscopy and evaluation for resistant infection or alternative causes. (1)
