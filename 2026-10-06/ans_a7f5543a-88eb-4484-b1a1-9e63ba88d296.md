 ## Stroke management in the ER

### 1. Immediate assessment and investigations
- Establish the **time of symptom onset** and perform an urgent neurologic assessment.
- Obtain immediate **noncontrast CT or MRI** to distinguish ischemic stroke from intracerebral hemorrhage; imaging should precede thrombolysis. (1) (2)
- Obtain blood tests including **CBC, PT/aPTT, glucose, troponin**, and an **ECG**. (1)
- For suspected anterior-circulation large-vessel disease, obtain **CTA or MRA**. Perfusion CT or diffusion-perfusion MRI can identify salvageable tissue. (1)

### 2. Initial supportive treatment
- Correct **hypoxia** when oxygen saturation is ≤94%.
- Correct **hypoglycemia** when glucose is <60 mg/dL; treat hyperglycemia toward **140–180 mg/dL**.
- Treat **hyperthermia** when temperature is >38°C and identify infectious causes. (1)
- Blood pressure is generally **not lowered acutely** in ischemic stroke unless it exceeds **185/110 mm Hg** in a potential thrombolysis candidate. Recommended IV agents include labetalol, nicardipine, or clevidipine. (1)

### 3. Acute ischemic stroke treatment
- **Intravenous alteplase:** administer within **4.5 hours** of symptom onset, or within **9 hours** when imaging shows diffusion-perfusion mismatch. Dose: **0.9 mg/kg**, maximum 90 mg; 10% as an IV bolus and the remainder over 60 minutes. Treatment should begin within 60 minutes of hospital arrival. (3)
- After alteplase, for the first **24 hours**, avoid anticoagulants and antiplatelet agents, closely monitor blood pressure, and avoid arterial puncture and placement of central lines, bladder catheters, or nasogastric tubes. (3) (4)
- **Intraarterial alteplase** may be considered up to **6 hours** in selected patients who are not candidates for IV thrombolysis or in whom it is unsuccessful. (4)
- **Mechanical thrombectomy** may be considered for proximal internal carotid or proximal middle cerebral artery occlusion, generally within **6 hours**, or within **24 hours** when diffusion-perfusion mismatch is demonstrated. (4)  
- **Table 13-5** summarizes maximum intervals: IV thrombolysis 4.5 hours without mismatch or 9 hours with mismatch; intraarterial thrombolysis 6 hours; thrombectomy 6 hours without mismatch or 24 hours with mismatch. (4)
- If thrombolysis is not being performed, aspirin is recommended for presumed noncardioembolic ischemic stroke: **325 mg once, followed by 81–325 mg daily**. (3)
- Anticoagulation with heparin followed by warfarin or another oral anticoagulant is indicated when a cardiac embolic source appears responsible. (3)

### 4. If intracerebral hemorrhage is identified
- Provide **airway support and ventilatory assistance** if required. (5)
- Treat hypertension with IV nicardipine and, if needed, labetalol, targeting systolic blood pressure of **140–179 mm Hg**. (5)
- Reverse coagulopathy with prothrombin complex concentrate or fresh frozen plasma; correct severe thrombocytopenia with platelet transfusion. Stop and reverse anticoagulant, thrombolytic, or antiplatelet effects when indicated. (5)
- Avoid both hypoglycemia and hyperglycemia, and treat fever with antipyretics. (5)
