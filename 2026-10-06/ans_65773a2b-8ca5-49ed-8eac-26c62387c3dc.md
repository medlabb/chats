## Answer

 **Apixaban 2.5 mg twice daily is used in these situations:**

- **Atrial fibrillation:** when the patient has **at least 2 of 3** criteria: age **≥80 years**, body weight **≤60 kg**, or serum creatinine **≥1.5 mg/dL**. (1)  
- **VTE prophylaxis after total hip or knee replacement:** typically for **35 days after hip replacement** or **12 days after knee replacement**. (2)  
- **Extended prevention of recurrent VTE:** after completing **6 months of therapeutic anticoagulation**, including in selected patients with cancer-associated VTE. (3)  
- **With certain strong P-gp/CYP3A4 inhibitors:** reduce from 5 mg twice daily to 2.5 mg twice daily when starting itraconazole, ketoconazole, or ritonavir; if already taking 2.5 mg twice daily, coadministration should be avoided. (4)

The selected references also mention this dose in hemodialysis patients in a small study, but do not establish it as a general standard indication. (5)
