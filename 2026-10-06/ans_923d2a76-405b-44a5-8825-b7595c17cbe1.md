```
What is hypereosinophilic syndrome
```

- - -

## Answer

 **Hypereosinophilic syndrome (HES)** is a group of rare disorders in which persistent eosinophilia—an abnormally high number of eosinophils in the blood and/or tissues—causes **organ damage or dysfunction due to eosinophil infiltration**. (1)

Diagnostic criteria generally include:

- Blood hypereosinophilia: an absolute eosinophil count of **≥1,500 cells/µL on two occasions at least two weeks apart**, and/or confirmed tissue eosinophilia; and  
- Documented organ damage or dysfunction attributable to eosinophils, after excluding other causes. (2) (3)

HES can affect nearly any organ system; skin, respiratory, gastrointestinal, and cardiac involvement are common. It may be **primary/neoplastic**, **secondary/reactive**, familial, idiopathic, or restricted to a single organ. (2)
