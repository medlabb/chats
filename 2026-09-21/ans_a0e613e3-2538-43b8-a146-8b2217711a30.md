## Answer

 **Aspirin dose depends on the indication:**

- **Usual maintenance dose:** 75–100 mg once daily; **81 mg daily** is common in the United States. (1, 2)
- **Typical loading dose:** 150–325 mg orally; use a non-enteric-coated preparation when rapid platelet inhibition is needed. (3, 4)
- **Higher doses:** 75–325 mg once daily may be used, but higher doses are generally not more effective and may cause more bleeding. (1)

Aspirin is contraindicated with **severe active bleeding** or a history of aspirin allergy with bronchospasm. (3)
