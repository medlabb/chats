## Answer

 **Amoxicillin duration for acute otitis media (AOM) in children:**

- **<2 years**, or **severe/recurrent AOM**, or **tympanic membrane perforation:** **10 days**. (1) (2)
- **≥2 years with mild-to-moderate, nonrecurrent AOM and an intact tympanic membrane:** **5–7 days**. (1)
- The AAP-described regimen is **10 days for <2 years, 7 days for ages 2–<6 years, and 5–7 days for ≥6 years**. (3)

The selected references do not provide a specific amoxicillin duration for adults.
