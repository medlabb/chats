## Answer

 Hospitalization or hospital-based treatment may be needed when herpes zoster (“zona”) involves:

- **Immunocompromised patients**, especially with severe disease—intravenous acyclovir is indicated. (1)
- **Disseminated zoster**: more than 20 vesicles outside the primary or adjacent dermatomes, particularly in an immunocompromised person. (2)
- **Possible visceral involvement**, such as hepatic, pulmonary, or central nervous system involvement. (2)
- **Serious complications**. (1)
- **Inability to take oral medication** or severe immunosuppression, when intravenous treatment may be required. (3)
