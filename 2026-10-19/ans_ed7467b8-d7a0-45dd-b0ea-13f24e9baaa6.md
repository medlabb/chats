## Answer

 **Paracetamol (acetaminophen) oral dose in children:**

- **10–15 mg/kg per dose every 4–6 hours as needed**
- **Maximum:** 5 doses in 24 hours and **75 mg/kg/day**, not exceeding **4,000 mg/day** (1)
- Include paracetamol from **all prescription, over-the-counter, and combination products** when calculating the daily maximum. Verify the liquid concentration before measuring the dose. (1)

**Common fixed oral doses** (weight-based dosing is preferred):  
- 0–3 months: 40 mg  
- 4–11 months: 80 mg  
- 1–2 years: 120 mg  
- 2–3 years: 160 mg  
- 4–5 years: 240 mg  
- 6–8 years: 320–325 mg  
- 9–10 years: 325–400 mg  
- 11 years: 480–500 mg  

Doses may be repeated every 4 hours, with a maximum of 5 doses/day; consult the product labeling, particularly for infants under 2 years. (2)
