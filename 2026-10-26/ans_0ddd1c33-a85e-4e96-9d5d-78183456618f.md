```
whats the first line treatment for dvt of the common femoral vein/
```

- - -

## Answer

 A **common femoral vein DVT is a proximal DVT**. For most patients without an absolute contraindication or prohibitive bleeding risk, the first-line treatment is **immediate therapeutic anticoagulation**. (rf_61) (1)

For hemodynamically stable patients, **oral factor Xa inhibitors—apixaban or rivaroxaban—are generally preferred**; LMWH, fondaparinux, or unfractionated heparin are alternatives in selected patients, such as those who are pregnant or have severe renal impairment. (2) Anticoagulation is generally continued for a **minimum of 3 months**. (3)

**Thrombolysis or thrombectomy is not routine** and is usually reserved for massive iliofemoral DVT, phlegmasia cerulea dolens, or failure of therapeutic anticoagulation. (4)
