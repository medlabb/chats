## Answer

 **Type 2 diabetes treatment—summary of the available reference**

- Treatment should be individualized. The reference recommends starting medication at diagnosis alongside weight management and exercise, rather than waiting to see whether lifestyle measures alone reach glucose goals. (1)
- If cardiovascular or kidney disease is present, the reference recommends a **GLP-1 receptor agonist or SGLT-2 inhibitor** as initial therapy. If neither is present, **metformin** is described as an effective, lower-cost first option. (1)
- If glucose remains above target, add another medicine—often a GLP-1 or GIP/GLP-1 receptor agonist or an SGLT-2 inhibitor—and consider further additions if needed. Sulfonylureas can cause low blood sugar and weight gain. (2)
- If combinations of non-insulin medicines do not achieve adequate control, insulin may be started; one approach described is adding long-acting insulin at bedtime and adjusting treatment as needed. (2)
- Weight management remains an important part of care. (3)

The cited treatment algorithm is based on **2022 ADA/European Association for the Study of Diabetes recommendations**; whether it reflects the latest guidance cannot be found in the selected references. (1)
