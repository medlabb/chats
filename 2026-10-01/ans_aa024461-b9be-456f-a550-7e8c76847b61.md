## Answer

 **Spirometry (FVC/FEV₁)**

- An acceptable forced maneuver should show a **sharp peak in the flow curve** and continue until exhaled volume reaches a plateau, or until expiration lasts **15 seconds**. For FEV₆, expiration should last **more than 6 seconds**. Inspect both the flow-volume curves and volume-time spirograms for poor technique. (rf_a8)
- Aim for **at least three acceptable maneuvers**. The two highest FVC values should agree within **0.15 L in adults** or **0.10 L in children**; the same limits apply to the two highest FEV₁ values. (rf_a8)
- Figure 2 illustrates curve inspection, Figure 3 relates to maneuver acceptability, and Table 1 presents the spirometry quality grading system. (rf_a8)

**DLCO**

- For an acceptable maneuver, inspired volume should generally be **at least 90% of the largest vital capacity**. It may be acceptable at **85% or more** if alveolar volume is sufficiently close to the highest value among acceptable maneuvers. At least 85% of the test gas should be inhaled in under 4 seconds, and breath-hold time should be **10 ± 2 seconds**. (1)
- At least **two acceptable tests** should agree within **2 mL/min per mmHg** (0.67 mmol/min per kPa). (1)

These references provide criteria for spirometry and DLCO; acceptability and reproducibility criteria for other PFT components cannot be found in the selected references.
