```
Murmur heard in mitral stenosis
```

- - -

## Answer

 Mitral stenosis produces a **low-pitched, rumbling mid- to late-diastolic murmur**, heard best at the **apex** with the patient lying in the **left lateral position**. In early disease, it is often preceded by an **opening snap**. In sinus rhythm, it may become louder just before S₁ (**presystolic accentuation**); this does not occur with atrial fibrillation. The murmur may become louder after mild exercise, and its duration generally reflects stenosis severity. (rf_65, 1)

In severe mitral stenosis with markedly reduced cardiac output, the murmur may be difficult to hear or absent. (rf_65)
