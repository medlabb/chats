## Answer

 **Acute coronary syndrome (ACS)** refers to suspected or confirmed acute myocardial ischemia or infarction. Its traditional types are **ST-elevation myocardial infarction (STEMI), non-ST-elevation myocardial infarction (NSTEMI), and unstable angina**. STEMI and NSTEMI involve a characteristic rise or fall in troponin; unstable angina describes suspected new or worsening ischemia without elevated biomarkers. (1) (2)

Most ACS is caused by a blood clot obstructing a coronary artery after an atherosclerotic plaque ruptures or erodes. Other causes include coronary artery dissection, spasm, and microvascular dysfunction. (3)

People with chest pain or symptoms such as shortness of breath that might be due to ACS should be evaluated rapidly for ischemia and other life-threatening conditions. (4)
