## Answer

 The selected references describe **ACC/AHA blood pressure categories** for adults: normal, <120/<80 mmHg; elevated, systolic 120–129 and diastolic <80; stage 1 hypertension, systolic 130–139 or diastolic 80–89; and stage 2, systolic ≥140 or diastolic ≥90. Classification should use the average of at least two readings on at least two occasions (1).

For treatment, the references recommend lifestyle measures for stage 1 hypertension without high cardiovascular risk; medication may be considered if BP remains above <130/80 mmHg after six months. Medication plus lifestyle measures is recommended for high-risk stage 1 hypertension and all stage 2 hypertension (2).
