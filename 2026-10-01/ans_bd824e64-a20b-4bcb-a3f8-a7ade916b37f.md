```
Dupilumab in atopic dermatitis
```

## Answer

 **Dupilumab** is a fully human monoclonal antibody that blocks the IL-4 receptor alpha subunit, inhibiting IL-4 and IL-13 signaling involved in atopic dermatitis (AD). It is approved in the United States and Europe for adults and children aged 6 months and older with moderate-to-severe AD inadequately controlled by prescription topical therapies; approval age differs in Canada. (1)

**Use and administration**
- In adults, treatment is given by subcutaneous injection: a **600 mg loading dose**, followed by **300 mg every other week**. Pediatric dosing is based on age and weight; for children aged 6 months to 5 years, dosing is every 4 weeks without a loading dose. (1; 2)
- Topical corticosteroids and other topical treatments are usually continued as needed. In patients whose disease is clear, the interval may sometimes be extended or treatment stopped; every-other-week dosing can be resumed if disease flares. (1)

**Efficacy**
- In two adult phase 3 trials, about **40%** of patients receiving dupilumab achieved clear or almost-clear skin at 16 weeks, versus about **10%** receiving placebo; EASI-75 was achieved by **44–52%** versus **12–15%**, respectively. (1)
- Trials in children also found greater improvement with dupilumab than placebo. For example, among children aged 6–11 years, EASI-75 at week 16 was achieved by **67–70%** of dupilumab-treated participants versus **27%** receiving placebo. (3)
- Dupilumab has also been shown to improve itch. (4)

**Safety and monitoring**
Common adverse effects include **injection-site reactions and conjunctivitis**; facial redness is also reported. Less common effects include psoriasiform dermatitis and arthritis or enthesitis. (3; 2) The cited pediatric reference states that **no laboratory tests are required** before or during treatment. (2)

Dupilumab is described as having a favorable safety profile and may be used long term, although cost and access may limit use. Contact-allergy testing may be needed in some patients before starting. (1)
