## Postnatal
Release of constriction rings can relieve venous congestion and lymphedema, and salvage some limbs [2,70,71]. In a review of one-stage circular incision techniques in 27 patients (average age 11.7 months [range 0 to 72 months]) with limb ring constrictions due to ABS, all limbs, toes, and fingers were saved [71]. The vascular compromise mandates urgent surgical intervention; otherwise, surgery can be delayed for weeks to months [72].
Postnatal treatment options include surgical interventions to enhance function or for cosmetic purposes (eg, Z-plasty) and prostheses and physiotherapy for individuals with limb abnormalities [73]. (See"Surgical reconstruction of the upper extremity".)
