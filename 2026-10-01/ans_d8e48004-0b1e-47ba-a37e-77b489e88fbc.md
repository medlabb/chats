## Answer

 **Anomic aphasia** can result from lesions anywhere in the **left-hemisphere language network**, including the middle and inferior temporal gyri. It is commonly seen with **head trauma, metabolic encephalopathy, and Alzheimer’s disease** (1). The clinical features are impaired naming and word-finding, with comprehension and repetition generally preserved (1, 2; Table 32-1).
