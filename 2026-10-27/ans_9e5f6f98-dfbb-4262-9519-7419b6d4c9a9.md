## Answer

 No. **Prednisolone clearance is dose-dependent**: total clearance increases by approximately **75% when the intravenous dose rises from 5 to 40 mg**. Therefore, a high-dose regimen does **not** have the same clearance as a low-dose regimen. Free prednisolone clearance also changes with dose, but to a lesser extent. (1)
