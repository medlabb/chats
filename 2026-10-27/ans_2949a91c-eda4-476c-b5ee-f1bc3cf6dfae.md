## Answer

 **Yes—meropenem 2 g IV every 8 hours can be given in adults for selected severe infections**, including meningitis, certain resistant gram-negative infections, and some critically ill patients. It may be administered by traditional infusion over 30 minutes or by extended infusion over 3 hours; extended infusion may improve target attainment in severe infection or when the pathogen has an elevated MIC. (1) (2)

**Renal function must be checked before using this dose.** For a usual 2 g every-8-hour regimen, the provided references list:  
- **CrCl >50 mL/min:** 2 g every 8 hours  
- **CrCl >25–50 mL/min:** 2 g every 12 hours  
- **CrCl 10–25 mL/min:** 1 g every 12 hours  
- **CrCl <10 mL/min:** 1 g every 24 hours or 500 mg every 12 hours (3)

The exact dose should therefore be based on the **infection, severity, organism susceptibility, and kidney function**.
