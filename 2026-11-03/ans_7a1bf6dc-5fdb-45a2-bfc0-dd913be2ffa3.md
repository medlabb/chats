## Answer

 **N-acetylcysteine (NAC) IV regimen:**  
- **Loading dose:** 140 mg/kg over 1 hour  
- **Then:** 70 mg/kg every 4 hours for 15–20 doses (1)

Treatment should ideally begin within **8 hours** of ingestion, though it may remain partially effective when started up to **24–36 hours** later. (1)
