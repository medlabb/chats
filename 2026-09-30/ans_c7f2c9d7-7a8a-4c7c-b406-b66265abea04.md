## Answer

 **A DSD assessment is specifically recommended if both testes are clinically nonpalpable:** the cited guideline advises immediate specialist consultation for phenotypic male newborns with bilateral nonpalpable testes. If one testis is palpable at the superficial inguinal ring, that exact presentation is not addressed by the guideline, so the ultrasound locations alone do not establish whether a DSD work-up is indicated. The absence of hypospadias and a normal SPL do not, from these references, rule out DSD; external genital appearance is rarely diagnostic. The references do not specify how normal SPL or an intra-abdominal testis should affect the decision in this situation. (1)
