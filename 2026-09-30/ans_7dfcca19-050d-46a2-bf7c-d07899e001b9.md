## Answer

 The interaction is **not purely theoretical at the pharmacologic level**: omeprazole may reduce clopidogrel activation/active-metabolite exposure and its antiplatelet effect. However, whether this translates into worse cardiovascular outcomes remains **uncertain and controversial**. The cited randomized trial found no significant difference in cardiovascular events, but it was too small to rule out a clinically meaningful difference. (1; 2; 3)

Recommendations on timing differ: one source advises taking the PPI in the morning and clopidogrel at least **4 hours later**, while another reports the manufacturer recommends avoiding omeprazole even when doses are **12 hours apart**. (1; 4; 5)

The references do **not** establish whether separating the doses improves clinical outcomes. (1; 4; 5)
