```
what are the associations of giant cell arteritis?
```

- - -

## Answer

 Giant cell arteritis is **frequently associated with polymyalgia rheumatica** and symptoms such as malaise, myalgia, weight loss, arthralgia, and fever. It is more common in women and Caucasians and is uncommon before age 60. (rf_08)

Other reported associations include **arteritic anterior ischemic optic neuropathy** and, less commonly, cranial nerve involvement—especially nerves III, IV, and VI. (1; 2)
