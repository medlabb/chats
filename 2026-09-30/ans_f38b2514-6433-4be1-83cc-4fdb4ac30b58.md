```
Newborn baby with bilateral undescended testis , on Usg one testis is localised at superficial inguinal ring other in paravesical space .  SPL is normal , no Hypospadias, should we have to work up for DSD ?!
```

## Answer

 **The key question is whether both testes are clinically nonpalpable.** Guidance recommends immediate DSD evaluation and multidisciplinary consultation for a phenotypically male newborn with **bilaterally nonpalpable testes**. (1, 2)

If one testis is palpable and the other is not, with no hypospadias or micropenis, the provided references do **not** establish that a comprehensive DSD work-up is required solely on that basis. Ultrasound locations do not by themselves clarify the clinical palpability criterion. (3)

So, confirm palpability on examination. If **both are nonpalpable**, proceed with prompt specialist assessment; the cited initial evaluation includes sex-chromosome testing, assessment for CAH, and evaluation of gonadal function at the appropriate time. (1, 4)
