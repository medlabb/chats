```
What is the antibiotics of choice for periorbital cellulitis?
```

- - -

## Answer

 For **periorbital (preseptal) cellulitis**:

- **Hospitalized or ill-appearing children:** **IV ampicillin–sulbactam** or **IV clindamycin plus cefotaxime/ceftriaxone** are recommended regimens. (1)
- **Well-appearing children with infected bites or traumatic wounds:** oral antibiotics targeting **S. aureus** and **group A Streptococcus** may be considered. (1)
- Empiric therapy should cover **MSSA, MRSA, S. pneumoniae,** and **group A Streptococcus**. (2)
