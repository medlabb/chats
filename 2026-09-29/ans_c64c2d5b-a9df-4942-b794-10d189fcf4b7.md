```
Pediatric dosing of Cefotaxim
```

- - -

## Answer

 - **Bacterial meningitis, children >1 month:** cefotaxime **225–300 mg/kg/day IV, divided every 6 hours**. (1)  
- **Sepsis excluding meningitis, previously well children with noncritical illness:** **75–225 mg/kg/day IV, divided every 8 hours**. (2)  
- For **preterm infants or infants <1 month**, the reference lists cefotaxime with ampicillin for empiric meningitis therapy but does not provide a cefotaxime dose. (1)

The doses cited assume **normal renal and hepatic function**. (1)
