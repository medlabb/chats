```
What is asthma COPD overlap syndrome ?
```

- - -

## Answer

 **Asthma–COPD overlap** refers to patients with airway-related symptoms that may result from the simultaneous coexistence of **asthma and chronic obstructive pulmonary disease (COPD)**. Many clinicians do not consider it a distinct syndrome, but use the term to identify this overlapping clinical presentation. (1)

In patients with both conditions, **smoking may reduce the response to inhaled corticosteroids**, and earlier use of anticholinergic therapy may be considered. (1)
