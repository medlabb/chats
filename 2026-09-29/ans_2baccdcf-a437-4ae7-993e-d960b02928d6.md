## Answer

 **Dupixent (dupilumab) is not described as first-line treatment** for chronic rhinosinusitis with nasal polyps (CRSwNP). The treatment sequence in the selected references is:

- **Initial treatment:** intranasal corticosteroids with saline irrigation. (1)
- **If symptoms remain uncontrolled:** functional endoscopic sinus surgery (FESS) is generally suggested before biologic therapy because it is less costly and provides immediate improvement. (1)
- **Place for biologic therapy:** a reasonable alternative to FESS in patients with severe or uncontrolled asthma who also require a biologic for asthma, those with another condition that could be treated by the same biologic, or those who cannot or do not wish to undergo surgery. (1)
- **After surgery:** biologic therapy is generally offered for recurrent polyps after FESS, particularly when the patient meets at least three of five criteria: type 2 inflammation, substantial systemic-steroid requirement or contraindication, major quality-of-life impairment, anosmia, and comorbid asthma. (rf_92)
- **Choice of biologic:** among the biologics discussed, dupilumab is suggested for most patients and has shown the greatest benefit in indirect comparisons; it may be particularly effective in aspirin-exacerbated respiratory disease. (2) (3)
