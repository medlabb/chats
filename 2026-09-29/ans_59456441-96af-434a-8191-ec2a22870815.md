```
What is the antibiotics of choice for periorbital cellulitis?
```

## Answer

 For **mild preseptal (periorbital) cellulitis** in an outpatient with no periorbital skin trauma, the preferred empiric antibiotic is **amoxicillin–clavulanate**. Alternatives include **cefpodoxime, cefuroxime, or cefdinir**; **levofloxacin** is suggested for life-threatening penicillin allergy. (1) (2)

If there is **periorbital skin trauma** and likely *S. aureus* infection, including community-acquired MRSA, use **linezolid alone** or **trimethoprim–sulfamethoxazole plus** amoxicillin–clavulanate, cefpodoxime, cefdinir, or cefuroxime. (1) (3)

**Clindamycin is not recommended for empiric treatment** because of substantial resistance. (3) Treatment is generally **5–7 days**, continued longer if erythema or swelling persists. (4) If there is proptosis, ophthalmoplegia, pain with eye movement, or concern for orbital involvement, the patient should be managed as possible orbital cellulitis rather than uncomplicated periorbital cellulitis.
