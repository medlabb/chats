## Answer

 **Behçet syndrome** (Behçet disease) is a rare, persistent, relapsing inflammatory multisystem disorder involving blood vessels of all sizes, on both the arterial and venous sides. (1)

Common manifestations include:

- **Recurrent oral aphthous ulcers**
- **Genital ulcers**
- **Eye disease**, including uveitis and retinal vasculitis
- **Skin lesions** and pathergy—a papular or pustular reaction after minor skin injury
- **Joint pain or arthritis**
- Possible gastrointestinal, neurologic, and vascular involvement, including thrombosis or aneurysms (2) (3)

Diagnosis is clinical: there are no pathognomonic laboratory findings. Commonly used diagnostic criteria require recurrent oral aphthae—three times in one year—plus at least two of recurrent genital ulcers, eye lesions, skin lesions, or a positive pathergy test. (4)
