## Answer

 **Supported interactions from the selected references:**

- **Azathioprine + mycophenolate:** May increase toxicity because both can cause bone-marrow suppression. Avoid coadministration when possible. (1)
- **Azathioprine + allopurinol or febuxostat:** This combination should generally be avoided because of the risk of severe leukopenia/bone-marrow suppression. If unavoidable, substantial azathioprine dose reduction and close white-cell-count monitoring are required. (2) (3) (4)
- **Mycophenolate + proton-pump inhibitors** such as pantoprazole or lansoprazole: May reduce mycophenolate exposure. The references do not specifically identify **vonoprazan** in this interaction. (2) (1)
- **Mycophenolate + aluminum/magnesium antacids, mineral supplements, or sevelamer:** May reduce mycophenolate absorption/exposure; separating administration by at least two hours is recommended in the references. (2) (1)
- **Mycophenolate + cholestyramine or other bile-acid sequestrants:** May substantially reduce mycophenolic-acid levels; avoidance is recommended. (1)
- **Mycophenolate + rifampin:** May significantly reduce mycophenolic-acid concentrations. (1)
- **Mycophenolate + acyclovir, valacyclovir, or probenecid:** May increase mycophenolic-acid concentrations. (1)

The selected references do **not provide supported interaction information** for the listed combinations involving **prednisolone, potassium chloride, vonoprazan, lubiprostone, ondansetron (“Dansetron”), or digoxin**, nor do they establish specific interactions among these drugs.
