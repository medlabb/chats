## Answer

 **Bronchiectasis** is a condition in which the airways become abnormally and often permanently widened and damaged. Mucus can build up and be difficult to clear, increasing the risk of lung infections. (1; 2)

Common features include a persistent or frequent wet/productive cough and, sometimes, shortness of breath, wheezing, coughing up blood, or chest pain. (2)

Causes include infections, airway obstruction, problems with immune defenses or cilia, cystic fibrosis, and some systemic diseases. (2)

Diagnosis is based on typical symptoms together with characteristic chest CT findings. CT is the defining imaging test; evaluation may also include blood tests, sputum testing, and lung-function tests. (2)
