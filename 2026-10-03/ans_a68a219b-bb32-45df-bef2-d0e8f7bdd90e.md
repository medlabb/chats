 ## Abstract

Liver diseases commonly present with **hepatocellular, cholestatic, or mixed** injury patterns. Initial assessment typically includes ALT, AST, alkaline phosphatase, direct and total bilirubin, albumin, and prothrombin time; results help characterize the injury and assess chronicity and severity. Ultrasound is the first imaging test when liver tests suggest cholestasis, particularly to assess for biliary dilation or gallstones. MRCP and ERCP visualize the biliary tree, with ERCP also offering sampling and therapeutic options. Primary biliary cholangitis is associated with antimitochondrial antibodies, while primary sclerosing cholangitis is diagnosed by cholangiography showing segmental strictures. Gallbladder contraction is stimulated by cholecystokinin (CCK); acalculous cholecystitis is associated with severe illness and settings such as prolonged fasting, trauma, burns, and major surgery.
