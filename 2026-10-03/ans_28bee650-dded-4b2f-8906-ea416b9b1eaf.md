## Answer

 **Dengue has no specific antiviral treatment; care is supportive**, with the goal of maintaining adequate fluid volume and recognizing severe illness early. (rf_79)

- **Mild illness:** Rest, drink plenty of fluids, and use **acetaminophen** for fever or aches. Avoid **aspirin and NSAIDs** such as ibuprofen or naproxen because they can increase bleeding risk. (1)
- **Monitoring:** The critical phase often begins as the fever subsides and can last 24–48 hours; patients may worsen quickly. Clinical review and blood-count monitoring may be needed, especially during this period. (2)
- **Seek prompt medical attention** for warning signs such as severe abdominal pain, persistent vomiting, bleeding, marked weakness or drowsiness, pale or cool skin, or difficulty breathing. (2)
- **Hospital care:** Hospital assessment is warranted for severe dengue or warning signs, and may be needed for people with coexisting risks such as pregnancy, infancy, older age, diabetes, or kidney failure. Treatment may include intravenous fluids; blood transfusion may be needed for significant bleeding. (2; 3)
