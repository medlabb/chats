## Answer

 ### Rheumatology MCQs

**1. Under the 2010 ACR-EULAR classification criteria, what score fulfills the requirements for definite rheumatoid arthritis (RA)?** (1, 2)

A. ≥3  
B. ≥4  
C. ≥6  
D. ≥8  

**Answer: C. ≥6.** A score of 6 or more fulfills the criteria for definite RA. (2)

---

**2. Which presentation is considered a red flag that may require urgent evaluation for septic arthritis or crystal arthritis?** (3, 4)

A. Chronic widespread pain without joint swelling  
B. Acute inflammatory monoarthritis  
C. Brief stiffness after inactivity  
D. Chronic focal tendon pain  

**Answer: B. Acute inflammatory monoarthritis.** It can indicate septic arthritis or acute crystal-induced arthritis, such as gout, and may require arthrocentesis or hospitalization if infection is suspected. (4)

---

**3. Which pattern of stiffness is more typical of an inflammatory disorder such as RA?** (5)

A. Brief stiffness, usually 45 minutes or less, that worsens with activity  
B. Stiffness lasting hours that improves with activity  
C. No stiffness, with pain only during exercise  
D. Intermittent stiffness that always indicates osteoarthritis  

**Answer: B. Stiffness lasting hours that improves with activity.** (5)

---

**4. How is involvement of four or more joints classified?** (6)

A. Monoarticular  
B. Oligoarticular  
C. Polyarticular  
D. Nonarticular  

**Answer: C. Polyarticular.** (6)

---

**5. Which joint-involvement pattern is typical of RA?** (6)

A. Asymmetric and oligoarticular  
B. Symmetric and polyarticular  
C. Always limited to the spine  
D. Focal and nonarticular  

**Answer: B. Symmetric and polyarticular.** (6)
