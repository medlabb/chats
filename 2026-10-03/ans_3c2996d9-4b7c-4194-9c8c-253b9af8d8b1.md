## Answer

 For **preeclampsia prevention during pregnancy**, the usual dose is **81 mg once daily**. Doses of **100–150 mg**, and possibly **162 mg**, may also provide benefit. It is ideally started at 12 weeks and before 16 weeks, then continued throughout pregnancy. (1)
