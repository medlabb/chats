## Answer

 The findings are most consistent with **opioid-induced central adrenal insufficiency** from chronic methadone use: long-term opioid therapy can suppress the hypothalamic-pituitary-adrenal axis and cause secondary adrenal insufficiency (1, 2). His low testosterone may also reflect opioid-induced central hypogonadism, which is more common than opioid-induced adrenal insufficiency (3).

Further evaluation may be needed: an ACTH stimulation test can be normal when opioid-related pituitary suppression is recent, so other testing or a trial of glucocorticoid replacement may be considered in that setting (3).
