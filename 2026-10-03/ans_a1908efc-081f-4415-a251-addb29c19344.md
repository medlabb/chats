## Answer

 **GAD treatment** commonly includes **cognitive behavioral therapy (CBT)** and antidepressant medication. CBT is effective, with approaches often combining relaxation training, cognitive therapy, and sometimes exposure or problem-solving. Internet-based CBT has also shown efficacy. (1)

For medication, **SSRIs and SNRIs are listed as first-line options** for GAD. Buspirone, tricyclic antidepressants, and benzodiazepines are listed as second-line options in the referenced monotherapy table. (2) Buspirone is used for chronic anxiety, primarily GAD, but may take time to reach its full effect. (3)

For **children and adolescents**, CBT and antidepressants are both effective; a large trial found that combined CBT and sertraline had higher response rates than either treatment alone. (4)

If treatment is not helping, the references recommend first checking the diagnosis, possible medical or co-occurring conditions, medication interactions, and whether the treatment trial had an adequate dose and duration. (3)
