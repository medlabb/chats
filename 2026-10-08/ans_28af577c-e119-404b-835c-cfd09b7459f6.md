What encephalopathy assessment tool should be used in assessing hie in preterm neonates ?

## Answer

 The **modified Sarnat scoring system** is used to clinically classify neonatal encephalopathy as mild, moderate, or severe. (1)

**EEG**, preferably multichannel continuous video EEG, is used to assess seizures and background activity; **aEEG** may be used when standard EEG is unavailable. (2)

The selected references do not identify a **preterm-specific encephalopathy assessment tool** for HIE.
