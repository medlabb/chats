```
Is pigtail considered risk for MRSA?
```

## Answer

 An **indwelling pigtail catheter may represent a healthcare-associated MRSA risk factor because it is an invasive device**; catheters can develop bacterial biofilms and provide a portal of entry for infection. (1) (2) (3)

However, the selected references do **not specifically identify pigtail catheters** as an independent MRSA risk factor.
