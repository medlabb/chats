## Answer

 The **modified Sarnat scoring system** is used to classify neonatal encephalopathy severity as mild, moderate, or severe, based on clinical findings including consciousness, activity, tone, posture, primitive reflexes, and autonomic function (1; 2).

However, the selected references do **not specify a separate or validated encephalopathy assessment tool specifically for preterm neonates**. EEG or aEEG may be used additionally to assess cerebral background activity and detect seizures (3).
