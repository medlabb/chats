How to adjust ventilator settings in a premature infant with RDS Type 1

## Answer

 In premature infants, **type I respiratory distress syndrome (RDS)** results from insufficient surfactant production by underdeveloped type II pneumocytes. Treatment described in the references includes **exogenous surfactant**, **corticosteroids to enhance lung maturation**, and mechanical ventilation with oxygen supplementation when needed. Mechanical ventilation and oxygen may contribute to bronchopulmonary dysplasia (BPD). (1) (2)
