```
What is the treatment of breast duct papilloma
```

- - -

## Answer

 Treatment of a **breast duct (intraductal) papilloma** depends on biopsy findings, symptoms, imaging agreement, and whether it is solitary or multiple:

- **Papilloma with atypia:** **Surgical excision is recommended**, because these lesions may be upgraded to ductal carcinoma in situ or cancer on excision. (1)
- **Papilloma without atypia:**  
  - If it is **small, incidental, benign, and imaging-pathology concordant**, close clinical and imaging follow-up may be appropriate.  
  - Excision is generally recommended if there is a **palpable mass, bloody nipple discharge, or imaging-pathology discordance**. (1)
- **Multiple or diffuse papillomas:** These may be managed definitively with **surgical excision**. (1)
- **Surgical options:** If discharge arises from one identifiable duct, **single-duct excision** can remove the papilloma while preserving other ducts. If discharge persists or comes from multiple ducts, **terminal or complete subareolar duct excision** may be considered for symptom relief. (2; 3; 4)
- **Pathology:** Any excised tissue should be sent for pathological analysis. (rf_16)

Duct excision can affect future breastfeeding and nipple sensation, so these potential effects should be discussed before surgery. (5)
