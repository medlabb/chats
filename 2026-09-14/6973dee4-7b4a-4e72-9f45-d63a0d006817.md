## Answer

 **Aspirin dosing depends on the indication:**

- **Primary cardiovascular prevention:** 75–100 mg once daily. (1)
- **Secondary prevention of cardiovascular disease:** commonly 75–100 mg once daily; doses up to 325 mg/day have been studied. (2)
- **Acute coronary syndrome:** 162–325 mg once initially, preferably chewed; maintenance is 75–100 mg once daily. (3)
- **Pain or fever:** 325 mg–1 g every 4–6 hours as needed; usual maximum is 4 g/day. (4)

The appropriate dose cannot be determined without knowing the indication.
