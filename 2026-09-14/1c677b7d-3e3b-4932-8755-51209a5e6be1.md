/q How long is a pilgrim’s Neisseria Meningitides vaccination certificate valid?

- - -

## Answer

 A pilgrim’s **Neisseria meningitidis (MenACWY) vaccination certificate is valid for five years**; boosters are recommended every five years if travel risk continues. (1)
