## Answer

 **OCD treatment:**  
- **CBT with exposure and response prevention (CBT/ERP)**, an **SSRI**, or **clomipramine** may be used; a combination may be needed. (1)  
- For **mild–moderate OCD in youth**, CBT-ERP is preferred initially. For **severe symptoms or an active comorbid disorder**, combined CBT and medication is suggested. (2)  
- If response is inadequate, options described include additional CBT, trying another SSRI, switching to clomipramine, or carefully supervised antipsychotic augmentation. (2)  

**If “BMD” means bipolar mood disorder with a depressive episode:**  
- Short-term antidepressant augmentation of an **antimanic medication** may provide small to moderate benefit, but evidence is mixed. (3)  
- **Olanzapine plus fluoxetine** is identified as the best-established antimanic/antidepressant combination for bipolar major depression. (3)  
- Another reference describes olanzapine plus fluoxetine after inadequate response to sequential quetiapine, lamotrigine, and lithium. (4)  

The selected references do **not** provide a complete treatment plan for OCD and bipolar disorder occurring together in a single episode.
