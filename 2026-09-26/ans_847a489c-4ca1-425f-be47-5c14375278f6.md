```
A term infant delivered via elective cesarean section presents with mild respiratory distress.

A chest X-ray  shows prominent perihilar interstitial markings, fluid in the minor (horizontal) fissure, and mild overaeration.

The symptoms resolve spontaneously within 48 hours.

What is the diagnosis?

A. Transient tachypnea of the newborn (TTN) 

B. Respiratory distress syndrome. 

C. Diaphragmatic hernia 

D. Lobar emphysema
```

- - -

## Answer

 **A. Transient tachypnea of the newborn (TTN).** It is associated with elective cesarean delivery, perihilar streaking, fluid-filled interlobar fissures, hyperinflation, and spontaneous resolution within 24–48 hours. (1) (2)
