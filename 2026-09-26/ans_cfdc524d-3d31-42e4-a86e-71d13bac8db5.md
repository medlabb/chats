## Answer

 After vigorous gardening—especially in **hot, humid conditions**—painful involuntary muscle spasms may be **heat cramps**. They commonly occur after heavy exertion, particularly in field workers, and may be associated with heavy sweating and replacing losses with large amounts of plain water. (1)

**Treatment:**
- Stop the activity and rest.
- If the cramps occur with heat exposure, move to a cooler environment.
- For heat cramps without significant dehydration, use a commercially available **electrolyte solution**. (1)
- Avoid swallowing undissolved salt tablets because they may irritate the stomach and cause vomiting. (1)
- For mild-to-moderate musculoskeletal pain, **acetaminophen or an NSAID such as ibuprofen or naproxen** may be effective. The referenced **Table 14-1** lists usual doses and intervals for these medicines. (2)
