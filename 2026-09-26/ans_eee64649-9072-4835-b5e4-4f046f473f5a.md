```
Group b streptococous screening should be done for pregnant women in which weeks of pregnancy?
```

- - -

## Answer

 **Group B streptococcus (GBS) screening** should be performed universally at **36 0/7 to 37 6/7 weeks of gestation**. (1)

Another reference states **35–37 weeks**. (2)
