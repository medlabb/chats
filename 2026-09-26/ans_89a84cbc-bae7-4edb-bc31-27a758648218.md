## Answer

 **Border zone** (or **penumbra**) refers to brain tissue with partially reduced blood flow—about **20–40% of normal**—where cellular damage may still be reversible if blood flow is restored. (1)

**Watershed/borderzone regions** are areas at the borders between the anterior, middle, and posterior cerebral arteries and are vulnerable during global cerebral ischemia. **Figure 13-22** illustrates the distribution of cerebral watershed (borderzone) infarcts. (2)
