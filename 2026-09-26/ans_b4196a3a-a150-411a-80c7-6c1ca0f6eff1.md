## Answer

 **Sclerema neonatorum** is most likely associated with a serious underlying illness rather than being caused solely by congenital heart disease. Reported associated conditions include **sepsis, dehydration, severe respiratory or gastrointestinal disease, intracranial hemorrhage, and congenital heart disease**. (1)

Its exact pathogenesis is **unknown**; proposed mechanisms include abnormal fat metabolism, connective-tissue dysfunction, systemic toxicity, and possible hardening of neonatal fat during shock or low temperature. (2)

The provided results (**TLC 19.6, Hb 9.5**) do not establish a specific cause. In this 3-day-old infant, **urgent neonatal evaluation for sepsis and other causes of critical illness is essential**, because sclerema is associated with high mortality and requires prompt treatment of the underlying condition.
