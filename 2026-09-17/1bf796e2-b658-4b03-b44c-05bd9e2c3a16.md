## Answer

 The statement is **not correct**. **Gilbert syndrome can contribute to neonatal unconjugated hyperbilirubinemia and may cause higher or more persistent jaundice in newborns**. However, the Gilbert genotype alone may not be sufficient; neonatal jaundice is more likely when combined with increased bilirubin production or conditions such as hemolysis. (1) (2)

Gilbert syndrome typically becomes clinically apparent during adolescence, so other causes of neonatal jaundice should still be evaluated. (3)
