## Answer

 That statement is **not completely correct**. Gilbert syndrome usually does not manifest until after the second decade, but **some neonates with Gilbert syndrome may develop unconjugated hyperbilirubinemia**. (1)

However, the *UGT1A1*28 variant alone, without G6PD deficiency, is generally not associated with significant hyperbilirubinemia. (1)
