```
“Gilbert syndrome does not cause neonatal jaundice.” Is this statement correct?
```

- - -

## Answer

 **No. The statement is incorrect.** Although Gilbert syndrome usually manifests after the second decade of life, **some neonates with Gilbert syndrome may develop unconjugated hyperbilirubinemia (neonatal jaundice)** due to diminished bilirubin uptake. (1)
