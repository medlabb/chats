normal respiratory rates and heart rate according to age

## Answer

 | Age/group | Normal respiratory rate | Normal heart rate |
|---|---:|---:|
| **Healthy newborns/infants** | Approximately **40–60 breaths/min**. During the **first hour after birth**, rates may be higher; about 50% of healthy newborns may exceed 60/min. (1) | **Term infants: 120–160 beats/min**; may decrease to about **85 beats/min during sleep**. (2) |
| **Newborn resting/sleeping range** | Not additionally specified. | **90–160 beats/min at rest**, with intermittent decreases as low as **70 beats/min during rest or sleep**. (3) |

A respiratory rate **greater than 60/min**, especially with retractions, nasal flaring, or grunting, is considered a sign of respiratory distress. (4)

Age-specific normal ranges beyond newborns/infants cannot be found in the selected references.
