## Answer

 ## Sunburn management

- **Start symptomatic treatment as soon as possible** after excessive UV exposure. Measures described include:
  - Increase fluid intake
  - Apply emollient creams or lotions
  - Use topical corticosteroids
  - Consider oral nonsteroidal or steroidal anti-inflammatory agents (1)

- For **first- and second-degree burns**, rinse the affected area with cool or room-temperature running water for **20 minutes**, then gently cleanse it. (2)

- As the redness resolves, **emollients** may help residual scaling; products containing **urea or a keratolytic agent** may also be beneficial. (3)

- Sunburn may cause **edema and blistering**; widespread reactions can be associated with chills and malaise, followed by itching and peeling over days to weeks. (1)
