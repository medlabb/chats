```
What’s the best diagnostic test for brucella
```

- - -

## Answer

 **Culture is the definitive diagnostic test and laboratory gold standard for brucellosis**, particularly culture from blood, normally sterile body fluid, or tissue. (1)

- **Bone marrow culture** is more sensitive than blood culture and may remain useful despite prior antibiotic treatment. (1)  
- In routine evaluation, obtain **blood cultures plus serologic testing**; automated blood-culture systems are preferred. (2)  
- Inform the laboratory when brucellosis is suspected so appropriate precautions and culture techniques are used. (2)
