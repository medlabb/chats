What are all the other tests for brucellosis?

## Answer

 Diagnostic tests for brucellosis include:

### **1. Culture**
- **Blood culture**—the laboratory gold standard, although sensitivity can be limited, particularly in chronic disease. (1)
- **Bone marrow culture**—more sensitive than blood culture and less affected by prior antibiotic use. (1)
- Culture of other specimens, including:
  - **Urine**
  - **Cerebrospinal fluid**
  - **Synovial fluid**
  - **Pleural fluid**
  - **Tissue**, such as bone marrow or liver biopsy (2)

### **2. Serologic tests**
- **Standard tube agglutination test (SAT)**
- **ELISA** for **IgM, IgG, and IgA**
- **Rose Bengal agglutination test**
- **Immunochromatographic lateral-flow assay**
- **Rapid latex agglutination test**
- **2-mercaptoethanol (2-ME) agglutination test**
- **Immunocapture agglutination test (Brucellacapt)**
- **Indirect Coombs test** (1) (3)

Serology may involve demonstrating a **fourfold or greater rise in antibody titer** between acute and convalescent serum samples collected at least two weeks apart. (2)

For complicated or chronic infection, combinations such as **SAT plus 2-ME, Brucellacapt, indirect Coombs, or ELISA for IgM and IgG** may be useful. (1)

### **3. Molecular tests**
- **PCR**, including:
  - Real-time PCR
  - Multiplex PCR
  - Nested PCR
  - PCR-enzyme immunoassay
  - Loop-mediated isothermal amplification (LAMP)
- **Brucella DNA detection** in a clinical specimen
- **16S rRNA gene sequencing** for genus identification; it cannot identify the species.
- **Bruce-ladder multiplex PCR** for identification and differentiation of *Brucella* strains. (3)

Molecular testing may be useful when serology is negative, but a positive result cannot distinguish active infection from previously resolved infection. (3)

### **4. Additional laboratory and targeted investigations**
- **Complete blood count**
- **Liver function tests** (4)
- **Synovial-fluid analysis and radiographic imaging** for suspected osteoarticular disease
- **Lumbar puncture** for neurologic manifestations
- **Bone marrow biopsy with culture and histopathology** when initial testing is unrevealing
- **Liver biopsy** when there is evidence of liver involvement and bone marrow evaluation is nondiagnostic (4)
- **MALDI-TOF mass spectrometry** for identification of *Brucella* species; this is described as a promising diagnostic method. (5)
