```
First line antibiotics in case of brucellosis
```

## Answer

 For **uncomplicated brucellosis in nonpregnant adults**, first-line treatment is **combination therapy for 6 weeks**:

- **Doxycycline for 6 weeks + streptomycin** for the first **14–21 days**, or  
- **Doxycycline for 6 weeks + gentamicin** for the first **7–10 days**.  

This doxycycline–aminoglycoside regimen is preferred because it has lower treatment-failure and relapse rates. **Doxycycline + rifampin**, both for 6 weeks, is an acceptable oral alternative. (1, 2)

**Monotherapy and courses shorter than 6 weeks are not recommended** because of high relapse rates. (1)

These regimens apply when there is no spondylitis, neurobrucellosis, or endocarditis; focal disease requires different and usually prolonged therapy. (2)
