## Answer

 **Aspirin dosing in children depends on the indication:**

- **Antiplatelet therapy:** **1–5 mg/kg/day** (1)
- **Acute rheumatic fever with typical migratory polyarthritis:** **50–70 mg/kg/day PO**, divided into 4 doses for 3–5 days; then **50 mg/kg/day** divided into 4 doses for 2–3 weeks, followed by half that dose for another 2–4 weeks (2)

Aspirin is generally **not recommended for routine pain or fever control in children**, particularly during viral infections because of the association with Reye syndrome (3).
