## Answer

 **Aspirin dose depends on the indication:**

- **Primary prevention of cardiovascular disease:** 75–100 mg once daily; 81 mg is a commonly available low-dose tablet. (1) (2)
- **Secondary prevention of cardiovascular disease:** usually 75–100 mg once daily; some selected patients may receive 325 mg daily. (3)
- **Suspected acute coronary syndrome/acute occlusive cardiovascular event:** 162–325 mg once, preferably a non–enteric-coated tablet that is chewed and swallowed. (4) (5)
- **Stable ischemic heart disease or peripheral artery disease:** 75–100 mg once daily for stable ischemic heart disease; 75–325 mg once daily when used alone for peripheral artery disease. (6)
- **Anti-inflammatory treatment for rheumatic arthritis:** 4–8 g/day in 4–5 divided doses, though adverse effects may limit use. (4)

The appropriate dose cannot be determined without knowing the **reason for aspirin use, age, bleeding risk, and other medications**.
