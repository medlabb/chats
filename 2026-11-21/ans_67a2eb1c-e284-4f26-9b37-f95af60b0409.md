## Answer

 **“Congruent and appropriate affect with mood”** means the patient’s observable, moment-to-moment emotional expression matches their sustained subjective mood and thought content. “Congruent” and “appropriate” are used as related terms for this match. (1)

For example, **depressed affect appropriate to a significant loss** would be considered congruent and appropriate. (2)
