## Answer

 For **preeclampsia prevention during pregnancy**, the usual aspirin dose is **81 mg once daily**. Emerging evidence suggests possible benefit with **100–150 mg daily**, potentially **162 mg**, but the selected references do not establish a single preferred dose. It is ideally started at **12 weeks and before 16 weeks**, continuing throughout pregnancy. (1)

Aspirin is **not routinely indicated solely for prevention of fetal growth restriction (FGR)** based on the selected references. (2)
