```
A 5-year-old child is brought to the emergency department 1 hour after a scorpion sting.

The child is restless and has profuse sweating, repeated vomiting, and rapid breathing.

On examination, there is tachycardia and hypertension.

What is the most likely explanation for these systemic manifestations?

A. Anaphylactic reaction

B. Autonomic storm

C. Septic shock

D. Acute adrenal insufficiency
```

- - -

## Answer

 **B. Autonomic storm**

Scorpion venom causes prolonged depolarization with excessive release of acetylcholine, epinephrine, and norepinephrine, producing combined parasympathomimetic and sympathomimetic effects. Vomiting and sweating reflect cholinergic excess, while tachycardia, hypertension, restlessness, and rapid breathing reflect sympathetic stimulation (1; 2).
