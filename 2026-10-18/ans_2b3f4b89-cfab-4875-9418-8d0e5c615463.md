## Answer

 **Intravenous lipid dose in neonates:**

- Lipids may be started at **3 g/kg/day**, without gradual stepwise increases. (1)
- Early lipid administration is recommended to reach **3 g/kg/day within the first few days of life**. (1)
- For extremely premature infants, intravenous lipids may be started at **3 g/kg/day with the first day of full parenteral nutrition**. (2)
- Infuse continuously over **24 hours**; lipid intake generally should not exceed **3–4 g/kg/day**. Infusion rates above **0.25 g/kg/hour** may be associated with decreased oxygenation. (1)
- Monitor triglycerides; maintaining concentrations **below 200 mg/dL** is described as prudent. (1)
